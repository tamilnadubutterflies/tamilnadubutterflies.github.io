About us

About The Nature and Butterfly Society

A Coimbatore-based conservation & citizen-science society systematically documenting Tamil Nadu's butterflies across the Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, wetlands, plains, and schools.

Society story & mission

Built by field naturalists across 38 districts

Geographically, Tamil Nadu spans a remarkable meeting ground where the Western Ghats and Eastern Ghats converge at the Nilgiri hills alongside fertile coastal plains and arid interior scrub. Yet before TNBS began compiling systematic records in 2012-2014, Tamil Nadu lacked an exclusive validated state checklist.

Registered formally in Coimbatore (Reg. No. 259/2018 (Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act, 1975)), TNBS coordinates field walks, Tamil Nadu Forest Department surveys, seasonal Danainae migration monitoring, school poster outreach, and the quarterly journal Pattampoochi.

Milestones (2012-2025)

How the society grew

  1. 2012 - 2014

    Base checklist compilation begins; Tamilnadu Butterflies Facebook group launched (02 Oct 2014) to gather verified sightings across districts.

  2. Jan 2015 - 2017

    Tamil Nadu Butterfly Checklist v1 prepared (323 species; 271 validated initially) and quarterly e-magazine Pattampoochi launched in Q1 2017.

  3. 2018 - 2019

    Registered as The Nature and Butterfly Society (259/2018); state butterfly discussions and steady expansion of verified checklist records to 325 species.

  4. 2021 - 2023

    Siruvani Hills documented as a butterfly super-hotspot; joint Tamil Nadu Forest Department surveys across Mudumalai, Sathyamangalam, Coimbatore, Erode, Salem, and Sirumalai.

  5. 2024 - 2025

    Tamil Nadu butterfly checklist reaches 329 species across 6 families (325 validated in TN), government school Tamil posters reach 13+ districts, and Butterfly Vision AI tops 923+ species.

Peer-reviewed research & publications

Original life-cycle discoveries, state checklist datasets, and computer-vision research authored by TNBS members.

  • Hari Theivaprakasham, Hari Ramanasaran & Appavu Pavendhan · Journal of Threatened Taxa, Vol. 13, No. 2 (26 Feb 2021), pp. 17722-17730

    First record of the early immature stages of the White Four-ring Ypthima ceylonica (Insecta: Lepidoptera: Nymphalidae), and a note on a new host plant from India

    Documents the egg, larval instars, and pupa of Ypthima ceylonica for the first time in India and records Cynodon dactylon (Bermuda grass) as a new larval host plant.
