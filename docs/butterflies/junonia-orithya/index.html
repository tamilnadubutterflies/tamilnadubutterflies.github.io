Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Nymphalinae (Pansies, Eggflies, Admirals & Oakleaf)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Nymphalinae): Familiar, colourful butterflies featuring conspicuous eyespots, seasonal morphs, and dramatic dead-leaf underside camouflage.

Typical habitats: Gardens, wetlands, agricultural bunds, scrublands, and hill forests.

Field photography tip: In Junonia pansies and Hypolimnas eggflies, both dorsal colour patches and ventral patterns aid rapid ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Sun-loving grassland pansy with electric-blue male hindwings

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males show velvety black forewings with cream bands and dazzling metallic sky-blue hindwings ringed by twin orange-edged eyespots; females are duller brown with larger eyespots.

Underwing: Cryptic sandy-grey and brown dry-grass camouflage.

Lookalikes: Electric blue hindwing contrasting against dark forewings cleanly separates it from all other Tamil Nadu Junonia pansies.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Dry grasslands, open lawns, bare rocky paths, scrub, and hill meadows.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread across plains, grasslands, and open hills in Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Common year-round, especially in sunny dry weather.

Flight & behaviour

Perches flat on bare sunlit earth or short grass to defend small sunny territories.

Field note: Territorial males fan iridescent azure hindwings repeatedly toward passing insects from bare ground perches.
