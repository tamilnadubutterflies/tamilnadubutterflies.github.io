Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Golden-yellow and black pied flat studded with crisp black-rimmed white spots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Warm golden-ochre ground crossed by black borders, a clear black sub-basal spot below the forewing cell, and crisp black-edged semi-translucent white cell, discal, and subapical spots; cilia are black-and-white chequered.

Underwing: Dark blackish-brown richly suffused with bright golden-yellow scales, throwing the crisp white hyaline spots into sharp relief; antennae are yellow with black clubs.

Lookalikes: Much brighter golden-yellow than Fulvous Pied Flat (Pseudocoladenia dan) and bears a prominent solid black spot near the base below the forewing cell.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Dry and moist deciduous forests, foothills, bamboo brakes, and shaded wooded gardens.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Mudumalai, Kallar, Siruvani, Coimbatore foothills, Anamalai, Pachamalai, Kolli Hills, Yercaud, and Tirunelveli foothills.

Seasonality: Active nearly year-round, most frequent from July to February.

Flight & behaviour

Flies fast near ground shrubs and rests flat on both upper and lower surfaces of sunlit or dappled leaves; visits bird droppings and low wildflowers.

Field note: Represented in South India by subspecies Coladenia indrani indra; dry-season broods tend to be noticeably brighter orange-yellow than wet-season individuals.
