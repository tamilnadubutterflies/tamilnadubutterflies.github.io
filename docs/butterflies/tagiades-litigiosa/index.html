Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Bold black-and-snow-white flat resting openly with spread wings

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark brown-black forewings specked with tiny white spots paired with hindwings whose lower half is snow-white with crisp black marginal dots.

Underwing: Almost pure snow-white across the hindwing.

Lookalikes: Cleaner snow-white lower half of the hindwing upper surface with discrete black marginal dots separates it from Common Snow Flat (Tagiades japetus).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist hill forests, wooded streams, and shaded foothills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Mudumalai, Siruvani, Kallar, Pachamalai, and Western/Eastern Ghats.

Seasonality: Common year-round in hills.

Flight & behaviour

Darts swiftly along shaded forest trails and settles flat underneath or atop broad leaves with wings spread.

Field note: Contrasting snow-white hindwing margin makes it flash brightly against dark shaded forest trails.
