Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Dusky small flat with blurred dark underside blotches common in shaded lanes

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark smoky grey-brown with tiny pin-point white hyaline subapical and upper cell dots on the forewing, faint darker discal bands, and brownish-grey chequered cilia.

Underwing: Paler grey-brown marked with large, soft-edged blackish-brown blotches across the forewing and faint greyish suffusion with obscure dark spot rows on the hindwing.

Lookalikes: Tiny forewing white dots and blurred dark underside blotches distinguish it from Spotted Small Flat (Sarangesa purendra), which has sharper white spots above and below.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Wooded gardens, moist and dry deciduous forest paths, bamboo brakes, and shaded road banks.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread across Tamil Nadu hills and foothills from Mudumalai, Siruvani, and Anamalai to Pachamalai, Yercaud, Kolli Hills, and wooded plains.

Seasonality: Common year-round statewide, peaking post-monsoon from September to February.

Flight & behaviour

Patrols low shaded earth banks and path edges, darting erratically before settling flat on soil, dry leaves, or beneath foliage; drinks from damp mud and small herbs.

Field note: Often rests pressed against shaded mud cuttings and tree trunks where its smoky grey-brown wings provide effective camouflage.
