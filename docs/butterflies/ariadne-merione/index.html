Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Biblidinae (Castors & Joker)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Biblidinae): Warm brown or orange-black butterflies with wavy transverse bands and slightly scalloped wing margins.

Typical habitats: Open scrub, gardens, castor stands, and dry deciduous foothills.

Field photography tip: Check wing margin contour (angled vs rounded) and hindwing sub-basal wavy lines. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Ubiquitous reddish-ochre castor fluttering around wild and roadside castor bushes

Identification marks

Upperwing: Rich reddish-ochre patterned with close-set, paired wavy dark brown transverse lines and a crisp white subapical spot on the forewing (stronger in wet-season morphs), with gently scalloped rather than sharply angled outer margins; females have slightly heavier bands.

Underwing: Warm rufous-brown crossed by alternating pale and dark purplish-brown wavy bands, showing stronger contrast in dry-season individuals.

Lookalikes: Distinguished from Angled Castor (Ariadne ariadne) by its smoother, less deeply indented forewing margin and closely paired, denser wavy dark lines across the upperside.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Roadway edges, agricultural fields, fallow plots, urban gardens, and open scrublands wherever castor grows.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Very common statewide across all Tamil Nadu districts from Chennai and coastal deltas to interior agricultural plains and ghat foothills.

Seasonality: Abundant year-round across plains and lower hills, increasing sharply after rains.

Flight & behaviour

Weak, low gliding flight around roadside castor bushes; settles flat on upper leaves in morning sun and visits small weeds like Tridax and Lantana.

Field note: Peninsular race Ariadne merione merione; often breeds abundantly on wild and cultivated castor (Ricinus communis), making it one of Tamil Nadu's most familiar roadside butterflies.
