Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

Paler four-lineblue distinguished by hoary grey-brown underside bands

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are dark purplish-brown overlaid with a pale silvery-violet gloss in side light and two white-edged dark hindwing spots in spaces 1b and 2; females are brown with a soft pale blue central forewing patch.

Underwing: Pale hoary grey-brown lacking sub-basal lines in the forewing cell (4-lineblue pattern), marked with finer, cleaner white transverse lines, a compact orange-ringed black spot in space 2, and a slender white-tipped tail at vein 2.

Lookalikes: Smaller and noticeably paler hoary grey beneath with narrower white bands than Large 4-lineblue (Nacaduba pactolus), and lacks the forewing cell sub-basal line pair present in Nacaduba kurava and other 6-lineblues.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist evergreen and semi-evergreen hill forests, stream banks, and shaded jungle clearings.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Occurs across Western Ghats slopes of Tamil Nadu including Siruvani, Kallar, Mudumalai, Anamalai, Palani Hills, Kalakkad-Mundanthurai, and Kanyakumari, with occasional records from wetter Eastern Ghats hills.

Seasonality: Mostly recorded from August to February during humid hill seasons.

Flight & behaviour

Hovers briskly around shaded forest foliage and joins mixed lineblue mud-puddling groups along damp gravel tracks.

Field note: Represented in southern India by subspecies Nacaduba hermus sidoma; males often blend silently into large puddling aggregations of 6-lineblues.

Caterpillar host plants

Entada rheedii (Elephant Creeper)
