Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

White-banded olive-brown flat glowing orange-yellow beneath the thorax

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark olive-brown forewing with three triangular hyaline white discal spots and a curved subapical arc; hindwing displays a broad creamy-white sub-basal band edged by a semicircle of black spots, paired with a white abdominal belt.

Underwing: Mirrors the crisp upperwing markings on a paler brown ground, contrasting strikingly with bright orange-yellow palpi and undersides of thorax and legs.

Lookalikes: White sub-basal hindwing band, white-belted abdomen, and vivid orange-yellow breast separate it instantly from Tagiades snow flats and Celaenorrhinus spotted flats.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist deciduous and evergreen forests, wooded ravines, and shaded stream-side trails.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Western Ghats and foothills of Tamil Nadu including Mudumalai, Siruvani, Anamalai, Palani foothills, and Kalakkad-Mundanthurai.

Seasonality: Recorded chiefly from August to March during monsoon and cooler winter months.

Flight & behaviour

Swift, low shaded flight; settles flat underneath leaves with wings fully pressed open and occasionally visits bird droppings and damp soil.

Field note: Its diagnostic orange-yellow breast is visible when the butterfly perches upside down beneath high leaves or feeds on vertical stems.

Caterpillar host plants

Dalbergia lanceolaria
