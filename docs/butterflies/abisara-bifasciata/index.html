Field guide notes for Riodinidae · Nemeobiinae (Judies (Old World Metalmarks))

Family overview (Riodinidae, Metalmarks): Small, forest-edge shade-loving butterflies represented in Tamil Nadu by two Plum Judy species (Abisara echerius and Abisara bifasciata).

Subfamily traits (Nemeobiinae): Purplish-brown butterflies with pale forewing bands and twin black submarginal eyespots near the hindwing angle.

Typical habitats: Damp shaded forests, foothills, bamboo thickets, and wooded gardens.

Field photography tip: Compare the angle and breadth of the pale median band across the forewing. Approach slowly along shaded forest paths and photograph the forewing white bands and hindwing twin spots.

Double-banded metalmark of dense moist forests in Tamil Nadu

Identification marks

Upperwing: Rich dark plum-brown crossed by two broader pale whitish bands towards the forewing tip.

Underwing: Deep purplish-brown with crisp contrasting median and post-discal white bands and twin black hindwing eyespots.

Lookalikes: Often treated alongside Abisara echerius; TNBS lists both species separately across Tamil Nadu forest surveys (including Mudumalai & Pachamalai).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist evergreen and deciduous hill forests and bamboo undergrowth.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Recorded in Western Ghats reserves (Mudumalai) and Eastern Ghats (Pachamalai).

Seasonality: Peaks during and after monsoon.

Flight & behaviour

Restless shaded flier turning abruptly after landing on leaves at eye level.

Field note: Represented regularly in TNBS Mudumalai and Pachamalai Eastern-Western Ghats joint surveys.
