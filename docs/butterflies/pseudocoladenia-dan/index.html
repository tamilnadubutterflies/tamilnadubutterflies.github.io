Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Warm rufous-brown flat common along sunlit forest paths and damp mud

Identification marks

Upperwing: Rich tawny rufous-brown marked with pale yellow-white semi-hyaline forewing spots (paired conjoined spots at cell end, discal spots, and three subapical dots) and wavy dark spot rows across the hindwing.

Underwing: Warm orange-brown repeating the dark hindwing bands and translucent forewing spots without crisp golden-black contrast; palpi are warm brown above and paler ochre beneath.

Lookalikes: Smaller and uniformly rufous-brown rather than bright black-and-golden-yellow like Tricoloured Pied Flat (Coladenia indrani), and lacks the bold black sub-basal forewing spot.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Forest edges, moist deciduous foothills, shaded gardens, coffee estates, and stream trails.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Abundant across Western Ghats (Mudumalai, Siruvani, Kallar, Anamalai, Palani Hills, KMTR) and Eastern Ghats (Yercaud, Kolli Hills, Pachamalai).

Seasonality: Common throughout the year across hills and foothills.

Flight & behaviour

Darts briskly at low height and perches flat on upper leaf surfaces, twigs, and damp mud; visits Lantana, Bidens, and bird droppings.

Field note: Nominate subspecies Pseudocoladenia dan dan flies in peninsular India; very accommodating to photographers when feeding at mineral-rich mud or flowers.
