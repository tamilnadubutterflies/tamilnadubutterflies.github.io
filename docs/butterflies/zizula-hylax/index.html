Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

Minute grass blue marked by twin black spots along the forewing costa

Identification marks

Upperwing: Tailless and dainty; males are bright violet-blue edged in a moderate dark brown border, while females are dull smoky brown with faint blue scales confined near the wing bases.

Underwing: Pale silvery-grey neatly dotted with crisp black spots: lacks a spot inside the forewing cell, carries two distinct black spots along the forewing costa (spaces 11 and 12), and shows an evenly curved hindwing discal spot row.

Lookalikes: Smaller and more delicate than Lesser Grass Blue (Zizina otis), and separated from both Z. otis and Dark Grass Blue (Zizeeria karsandra) by the two clear black spots along the forewing costa.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Roadside weeds, gardens, wetland margins, open scrub, and sunlit forest edges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread across all districts of Tamil Nadu from coastal scrub and urban parks to Western and Eastern Ghats foothills.

Seasonality: Common year-round statewide.

Flight & behaviour

Weak, dancing flight inches over low herbs and marsh edges; perches delicately on grass stems and Acanthaceae flowers.

Field note: Among the smallest butterflies in India; unlike typical legume-feeding grass blues, its caterpillars feed largely on Acanthaceae buds and Lantana florets.
