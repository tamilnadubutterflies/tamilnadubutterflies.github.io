Field guide notes for Pieridae · Pierinae (Whites, Orange-tips & Jezebels)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Pierinae): Includes Delias jezebels, Cepora gulls, Appias albatrosses/puffins, Colotis/Ixias orange-tips, Hebomoia, and Prioneris.

Typical habitats: Spans dry scrub plains (Colotis), urban avenues (Delias), and wet evergreen hills (Appias, Prioneris).

Field photography tip: Capture forewing tip markings and underside hindwing vein patterns. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

Tiny dry-scrub white bearing a compact black-bordered orange tip

Identification marks

Upperwing: Chalky white with a neat oval orange forewing apex entirely enclosed by a black rim and a small black spot at the cell end; hindwing shows marginal black dots.

Underwing: Greenish-yellow hindwing speckled finely in grey with a faint discal row of brownish dots and dull orange showing softly through the forewing tip.

Lookalikes: Noticeably smaller and whiter than Yellow Orange-tip, and unlike Plain Orange-tip (Colotis aurora), its black-edged orange tip contains zero isolated white spots.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Arid thorn scrub, coastal dunes, wasteland paths, and dry plains.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Ubiquitous across dry Tamil Nadu lowlands, Vellalore wetlands, Madurai plains, and Coromandel Coast.

Seasonality: Common year-round in dry plains, especially abundant post-northeast monsoon.

Flight & behaviour

Weak, low-skimming flight among dry bushes and low weeds like Tridax and Justicia.

Field note: One of the smallest members of Pieridae regularly seen in dry urban wastelands across Tamil Nadu.
