Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Theclinae (Silverlines, Oakblues, Royals, Flashes & Yamfly)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Theclinae): Often tailed hairstreaks with metallic silver bars (Cigaritis), deep violet-blue upperwings (Arhopala), or swift red-and-slate flashes (Rapala).

Typical habitats: Scrub jungles, forest canopies, mistletoe-bearing trees, and rocky hilltops.

Field photography tip: Capture the hindwing tornal lobes, tail count, and arrangement of underside bands. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

Widespread orange-banded silverline of sunny scrub, gardens, and dry hills

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark brown with a faint violet sheen in fresh males, marked on the forewing with three to four bright orange-red transverse bands descending from the costa; females are slightly larger and rounder with paler, broader orange bands and an orange-red tornal patch on the hindwing.

Underwing: Pale greyish-buff to cream-yellow crossed by clean, evenly spaced rusty-red to ochreous bars edged finely in black and centered with crisp metallic silver lines; bears two white-tipped black tails and a double black-spotted orange tornal lobe.

Lookalikes: Unlike Plumbeous Silverline (Cigaritis schistacea), lacks the bright blue male hindwing gloss and shows wider pale gaps between its cleaner ventral silver bars; Common Shot Silverline (Cigaritis ictis) shows a single fused triangular orange forewing patch rather than three separate orange bands.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Dry scrub jungles, thorny hedges, urban parks, open plains, roadside Lantana patches, and deciduous foothills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Statewide from Chennai coastal scrub, Guindy, and Point Calimere across Coimbatore, Madurai, Salem, Pachamalai, and Western Ghats foothills.

Seasonality: Active year-round across Tamil Nadu, most abundant after northeast monsoon showers from October to February.

Flight & behaviour

Low, darting flier that basks with half-open wings on sunny twigs, visits Tridax and Chromolaena blooms, and oscillates its hindwings to animate the orange tornal false head.

Field note: By far Tamil Nadu's commonest silverline; females lay eggs only near Crematogaster ant trails, and caterpillars feed on tender shoots while guarded inside ant-attended leaf shelters.
