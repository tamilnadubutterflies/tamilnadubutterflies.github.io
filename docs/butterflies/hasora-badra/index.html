Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Coeliadinae (Awls, Awlets & Awlkings)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Coeliadinae): Large, swift skippers active particularly at dawn, dusk, and shaded forest interiors; several possess metallic-green or bright orange markings.

Typical habitats: Dense hill forests, bamboo patches, shaded ravines, and coastal scrub.

Field photography tip: Focus on the underside hindwing white/blue bands and dark tornal spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Large brown awl awaiting validated photographic record in Tamil Nadu

Identification marks

Upperwing: Plain dark brown in males; females bear large pale yellow hyaline spots in the forewing.

Underwing: Warm purplish-brown lacking the crisp white hindwing band of Common or White Banded Awl, showing instead a small dark-ringed white spot at the cell end and dark tornal lobe.

Lookalikes: Lacks the continuous pale hindwing underside band seen in Hasora chromus and Hasora taminatus.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Shaded evergreen forests and wooded hill ravines.

Tamil Nadu distribution: One of 4 checklist species awaiting validated TNBS sighting confirmation in Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Monsoon and post-monsoon months.

Flight & behaviour

Crepuscular flier sheltering beneath leaves during midday.

Field note: Check crepuscular Derris vine thickets in wet Western Ghats valleys for the stark white underside hindwing cell spot.
