Field guide notes for Pieridae · Pierinae (Whites, Orange-tips & Jezebels)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Pierinae): Includes Delias jezebels, Cepora gulls, Appias albatrosses/puffins, Colotis/Ixias orange-tips, Hebomoia, and Prioneris.

Typical habitats: Spans dry scrub plains (Colotis), urban avenues (Delias), and wet evergreen hills (Appias, Prioneris).

Field photography tip: Capture forewing tip markings and underside hindwing vein patterns. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

Dry-zone white pierid with bright orange forewing patches framed in black

Identification marks

Upperwing: Chalky white with a broad vivid orange patch occupying the outer half of the forewing, enclosed inside a wide black margin.

Underwing: Rich sulphur-yellow speckled with brownish spots and small white-centered eyespots.

Lookalikes: Distinguished from Yellow Orange-tip (Ixias pyrene) by its white ground colour above (instead of bright yellow).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Dry scrub, thorny jungles, open plains, and dry deciduous foothills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Common across dry lowlands, Salem, Madurai, Tirunelveli, and Eastern Ghats foothills.

Seasonality: Year-round, particularly numerous in dry eastern and interior plains.

Flight & behaviour

Quick, low erratic flight through thorny caper bushes.

Field note: Shows dramatic dry-season polymorphism with rich reddish eyespot-like speckles beneath.
