Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Nymphalinae (Pansies, Eggflies, Admirals & Oakleaf)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Nymphalinae): Familiar, colourful butterflies featuring conspicuous eyespots, seasonal morphs, and dramatic dead-leaf underside camouflage.

Typical habitats: Gardens, wetlands, agricultural bunds, scrublands, and hill forests.

Field photography tip: In Junonia pansies and Hypolimnas eggflies, both dorsal colour patches and ventral patterns aid rapid ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Montane velvety-black admiral slashed with vivid scarlet-orange bands

Identification marks

Upperwing: Deep velvety black-brown slashed diagonally across the forewing by a vivid scarlet-orange band bearing black blotches, crisp white subapical spots, and a matching scarlet-orange hindwing border dotted with black; sexes look alike.

Underwing: Forewing repeats the scarlet band and white spots with a metallic blue ring in the cell; hindwing shows an intricate dark camouflage mosaic of smoky brown, grey, white, and fine black marbling.

Lookalikes: Easily separated from Painted Lady (Vanessa cardui) by its dark velvety ground colour and vivid scarlet-orange forewing band and hindwing border.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: High-elevation montane shola edges, hill-station gardens, grassy ridges, and rocky summit clearings above 1,200 m.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Confined to high hills of Tamil Nadu, chiefly the Nilgiris (Ooty, Coonoor, Kotagiri, Mukurthi), Palani Hills (Kodaikanal), and high Anamalai-Meghamalai ridges.

Seasonality: Active throughout the year on upper plateaus, peaking between February and May and September to December.

Flight & behaviour

Fast, powerful flier patrolling sunny hill ridges and Lantana or garden borders; perches aggressively on sunlit paths and rocky outcrops with open wings.

Field note: Peninsular India holds the endemic southern subspecies Vanessa indica pholoe; caterpillars construct folded leaf shelters out of stinging Girardinia nettles.
