Field guide notes for Pieridae · Pierinae (Whites, Orange-tips & Jezebels)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Pierinae): Includes Delias jezebels, Cepora gulls, Appias albatrosses/puffins, Colotis/Ixias orange-tips, Hebomoia, and Prioneris.

Typical habitats: Spans dry scrub plains (Colotis), urban avenues (Delias), and wet evergreen hills (Appias, Prioneris).

Field photography tip: Capture forewing tip markings and underside hindwing vein patterns. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

Delicate salmon-pink pierid dancing over coastal and arid scrublands

Identification marks

Upperwing: Soft salmon-pink framed by wide black wing borders containing tiny pink marginal spots; forewing carries a prominent black dot at the cell end (females may be whitish or yellowish).

Underwing: Soft greenish-yellow suffused subtly at the forewing base with pale pink and bearing faint transverse dark dots.

Lookalikes: Smaller and distinctly bordered in spotted black compared to the warmer orange Large Salmon Arab (Colotis fausta).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Coastal sand dunes, thorny dry scrub plains, saltmarsh edges, and Salvadora thickets.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Dry plains and east coast belt of Tamil Nadu including Point Calimere, Thoothukudi, Ramanathapuram, Tirunelveli, and drier Coimbatore scrub.

Seasonality: Active throughout the year in coastal and rain-shadow scrub tracts, peaking October to March.

Flight & behaviour

Stays close to thorny Salvadora bushes, fluttering weakly in small colonies and hiding inside foliage during windy afternoons.

Field note: Closely tied to Toothbrush Tree (Salvadora persica), making it a reliable bioindicator of healthy coastal and arid thorn scrub.
