Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Limenitidinae (Sailers, Sergeants, Lascars, Barons & Clipper)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Limenitidinae): Gliding butterflies with characteristic black-and-white banded wings (Neptis, Athyma) or emerald-olive camouflaged upperwings (Euthalia, Dophla).

Typical habitats: Forest trails, stream banks, orchards, and wooded gardens.

Field photography tip: Check whether the forewing cell streak is broken into spots or continuous, and compare underside ground colour. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Heavy-bodied woodland sailer marked by a short broad hindwing white band

Identification marks

Upperwing: Charcoal-black with broad pure white markings; the forewing carries a short narrow cell streak followed by a large detached spot, an elongated basal white streak in space 1a, and a broad hindwing white band stopping short of the costal border.

Underwing: Rich golden-ferruginous brown echoing the broad white dorsal bands, bordered outwardly by diffuse pale submarginal lunules and dark marginal dots.

Lookalikes: Larger and heavier-winged than Common Sailer (Neptis hylas), and told from Chestnut-streaked Sailer (Neptis jumbah) by the broad, abruptly shortened white hindwing band, very narrow forewing cell streak, and absence of dark chestnut discal spots underneath.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist deciduous and semi-evergreen hill forests, wooded foothills, shaded streams, and orchard edges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Western Ghats foothills and hills (Kovai Kutralam, Siruvani, Kallar, Mudumalai, Anamalai, KMTR) plus wooded Eastern Ghats hills.

Seasonality: Recorded across most months in hill forests, peaking post-monsoon from October to January.

Flight & behaviour

Powerful, deliberate glider sailing along canopy margins and descending to mud-puddle on wet paths or feed on Lantana and sap drips.

Field note: Documented on Coimbatore nature walks at Kovai Kutralam; structurally separated from Neptis by hindwing vein 8 ending on the outer margin below the apex.
