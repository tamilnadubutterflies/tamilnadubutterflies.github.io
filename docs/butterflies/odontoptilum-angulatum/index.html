Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Warm chestnut-brown skipper with wavy wing edges and chalky white under-hindwings

Identification marks

Upperwing: Rich chestnut-brown crossed by darker discal bands, pale crescentic hyaline forewing marks, and fine wavy whitish striae across an angled, tooth-edged hindwing.

Underwing: Forewing mottled brown with pale violet-grey suffusion; hindwing starkly chalky white washed thinly with pale brown bands and dark outer specks, flashing bright white in flight.

Lookalikes: Rich chestnut upperside, deeply angled hindwing margin at vein 7, and chalky white under-hindwing separate it from Caprona angles and Tapena thwaitesi.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist and dry deciduous forests, foothills, bamboo-lined paths, and riparian scrub.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread in Western and Eastern Ghats foothills including Kallar, Siruvani, Mudumalai, Anamalai, Pachamalai, Kolli Hills, and Yercaud.

Seasonality: Recorded year-round, peaking from July to January.

Flight & behaviour

Basks completely flat on sunny upper leaf surfaces or rocky stream beds, often sipping damp mineral water and bird droppings before darting into shade.

Field note: Males carry a prominent white hair-tuft on the forelegs while females bear a thick anal hair tuft used to cloak freshly laid eggs.
