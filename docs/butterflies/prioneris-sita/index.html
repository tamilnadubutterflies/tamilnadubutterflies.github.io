Field guide notes for Pieridae · Pierinae (Whites, Orange-tips & Jezebels)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Pierinae): Includes Delias jezebels, Cepora gulls, Appias albatrosses/puffins, Colotis/Ixias orange-tips, Hebomoia, and Prioneris.

Typical habitats: Spans dry scrub plains (Colotis), urban avenues (Delias), and wet evergreen hills (Appias, Prioneris).

Field photography tip: Capture forewing tip markings and underside hindwing vein patterns. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

Striking Jezebel mimic bearing serrated forewings and vermilion underside spots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Large stark-white wings with every vein crisply traced in velvety black along the outer half; forewing costa shows minute comb-like serrations on males.

Underwing: Bright golden-yellow hindwings framed by thick black veins and a bold submarginal chain of brilliant vermilion-red spots.

Lookalikes: Spectacular Batesian mimic of Common Jezebel (Delias eucharis), separated by being larger, pure white above with black veins on the forewing, and having a fine inner-margin edge to the red spots.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Wet evergreen hill forests, canopy glades, and high-rainfall Western Ghats ridges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Wet Western Ghats hills of Tamil Nadu: Nilgiris, Anamalai, Valparai, Siruvani, and KMTR.

Seasonality: Peak sightings between October and April across evergreen hill ranges.

Flight & behaviour

Strong, rapid flier around high forest canopies and hilltops, occasionally dropping to stream seepages or flowers.

Field note: True Common Jezebels feed on mistletoes (toxic) and fly slowly, whereas Painted Sawtooth feeds on non-toxic Capparis and mimics Jezebels for predator protection.
