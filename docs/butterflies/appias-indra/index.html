Field guide notes for Pieridae · Pierinae (Whites, Orange-tips & Jezebels)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Pierinae): Includes Delias jezebels, Cepora gulls, Appias albatrosses/puffins, Colotis/Ixias orange-tips, Hebomoia, and Prioneris.

Typical habitats: Spans dry scrub plains (Colotis), urban avenues (Delias), and wet evergreen hills (Appias, Prioneris).

Field photography tip: Capture forewing tip markings and underside hindwing vein patterns. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

Fast white puffin patterned with a solid black forewing tip containing white dots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Pointed white forewings bearing a wide black triangular outer tip pierced by two small white apical spots; hindwings are white with tiny marginal black speckles in wet broods.

Underwing: Hindwings pale yellow to heavily mottled greyish-brown in wet-season forms; forewing apex is marbled grey-ochre bordered inwardly by an angled black bar.

Lookalikes: Two distinct white spots enclosed inside an unbroken black forewing apex and a heavy black underside subapical bar distinguish it from Appias albatrosses.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Evergreen and moist deciduous hill forests, forested ghats, and mountain streams.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Nilgiris, Mudumalai, Siruvani, Kallar, Valparai, and southern Western Ghats hills.

Seasonality: Seen primarily between July and February across wet hill forests.

Flight & behaviour

Powerful hill flyer often joining mud-puddling congregations on wet gravel near Kallar and Mudumalai streams.

Field note: Documented as a survey highlight in the June 2024 Mudumalai Tiger Reserve Monsoon Butterfly Survey.
