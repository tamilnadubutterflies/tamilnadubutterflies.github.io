Field guide notes for Riodinidae · Nemeobiinae (Judies (Old World Metalmarks))

Family overview (Riodinidae, Metalmarks): Small, forest-edge shade-loving butterflies represented in Tamil Nadu by two Plum Judy species (Abisara echerius and Abisara bifasciata).

Subfamily traits (Nemeobiinae): Purplish-brown butterflies with pale forewing bands and twin black submarginal eyespots near the hindwing angle.

Typical habitats: Damp shaded forests, foothills, bamboo thickets, and wooded gardens.

Field photography tip: Compare the angle and breadth of the pale median band across the forewing. Approach slowly along shaded forest paths and photograph the forewing white bands and hindwing twin spots.

Shaded woodland metalmark common across Western and Eastern Ghats

Identification marks

Upperwing: Velvety maroon-brown wings crossed by pale greyish-white subapical bands and bearing dark twin spots near the hindwing margin.

Underwing: Warm purplish-brown displaying crisp white diagonal median bands and black white-ringed submarginal eyespots.

Lookalikes: Closely resembles Plum Judy (Abisara bifasciata); Straight Plum Judy typically shows narrower, straighter median bands and habitat preference across peninsular hills.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Shaded forest trails, bamboo groves, overgrown estates, and stream sides.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread in Tamil Nadu hill forests and foothills.

Seasonality: Year-round, most common August–January.

Flight & behaviour

Bobs jerkily through forest shade, settling with half-open wings on upper leaf surfaces and pivoting smoothly right after landing.

Field note: Abrupt quarter-turn upon landing is a reliable behavioral clue along shaded forest undergrowth.
