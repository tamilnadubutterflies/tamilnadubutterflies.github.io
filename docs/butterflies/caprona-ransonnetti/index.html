Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Dry-zone spotted flat breeding openly on Urena lobata foliage

Identification marks

Upperwing: Warm golden-brown wings dappled with translucent white forewing spots and pale scalloped outer margins.

Underwing: Chalky pale creamy-white speckled with small brown dots on the outer half, strongly contrasting with its brown upperside.

Lookalikes: Bright chalky-white underside with crisp brown marginal spots separates Golden Angle from Spotted Angle (Caprona agama).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Scrub forests, dry deciduous foothills, open sunny trails, and roadside Urena thickets.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread across plains, scrub hills, and Western/Eastern Ghats foothills.

Seasonality: Year-round, peaking post-monsoon.

Flight & behaviour

Rests flat with wings wide open on sunlit leaves or underneath leaf blades; caterpillars cut and fold neat leaf shelters.

Field note: Pale yellow-green caterpillars feed openly during bright sunshine on Urena lobata shrubs, documented by TNBS photographers.

Caterpillar host plants
