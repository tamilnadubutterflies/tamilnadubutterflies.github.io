Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Coeliadinae (Awls, Awlets & Awlkings)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Coeliadinae): Large, swift skippers active particularly at dawn, dusk, and shaded forest interiors; several possess metallic-green or bright orange markings.

Typical habitats: Dense hill forests, bamboo patches, shaded ravines, and coastal scrub.

Field photography tip: Focus on the underside hindwing white/blue bands and dark tornal spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

Fast twilight-loving awl with a crisp white hindwing underside band

Identification marks

Upperwing: Plain dark glossy brown with an orange-brown sheen at wing bases; females show two pale yellow translucent forewing spots.

Underwing: Deep purplish-brown crossed cleanly on the hindwing by a narrow pure-white straight band ending at a dark tornal patch.

Lookalikes: Narrow, clean straight white hindwing band (without metallic green tint) separates it from White Banded Awl (Hasora taminatus).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Avenues, wooded gardens, foothills, and forest clearings.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread from Chennai and Coimbatore avenues to Mudumalai and Pachamalai.

Seasonality: Common year-round statewide.

Flight & behaviour

Most active at dawn, dusk, and in overcast weather; settles under leaves during bright midday sun.

Field note: Life cycle documented by TNBS contributors on Pongamia pinnata street trees in urban Tamil Nadu.
