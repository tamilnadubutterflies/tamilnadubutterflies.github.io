Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Danainae (Tigers, Crows & Tree Nymphs)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Danainae): Tough, unpalatable butterflies whose caterpillars feed on milkweeds and dogbanes; central to Tamil Nadu’s seasonal Eastern–Western Ghats migrations.

Typical habitats: Hills, plains, urban parks, and forest corridors throughout Tamil Nadu.

Field photography tip: Count subapical spots and pale streaks in the forewing cell to separate Blue Tiger vs Dark Blue Tiger and the Crow species. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Key long-distance migrant across Western and Eastern Ghats corridors

Identification marks

Upperwing: Deep black wings patterned with translucent pale turquoise-blue streaks, rounded spots, and paired basal forewing stripes.

Underwing: Golden-brown underside mirroring the pale blue markings; males bear a small scent pouch on the hindwing.

Lookalikes: Wider, paler blue streaks and rounded forewing spots distinguish it from the darker, narrower-streaked Dark Blue Tiger (Tirumala septentrionis).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Scrublands, parks, hill forests, wetlands, and seasonal migratory flyways.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Statewide; flagship species of TNBS’s 12+ years of Tamil Nadu migration monitoring.

Seasonality: Year-round; huge directional aggregations recorded by TNBS in Sep–Nov and Apr–May.

Flight & behaviour

Glides tirelessly along fixed directional flyways between the Eastern and Western Ghats during April–May and September–November.

Field note: Tracked annually by TNBS from Yercaud/Salem across Tiruppur and Coimbatore toward the Nilgiris and Anamalais.
