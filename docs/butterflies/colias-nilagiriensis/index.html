Field guide notes for Pieridae · Coliadinae (Yellows, Emigrants & Clouded Yellows)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Coliadinae): Yellow, pale green, or orange butterflies with rapid sustained flight and strong migratory behaviour in several species.

Typical habitats: Open grasslands, agricultural margins, scrub, riverbeds, and Nilgiri/Palani high-altitude meadows (Colias).

Field photography tip: For Eurema grass yellows, zoom into the underside forewing cell to count the small dark cell spots. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

High-altitude clouded yellow endemic to Nilgiri and Palani shola-grasslands

Identification marks

Upperwing: Golden-yellow (pale greenish-white in some females) bordered by broad dark brown-black outer bands dotted with yellow spots.

Underwing: Ochre-yellow dusted greenish with a crisp silver-white centered pink-ringed hindwing spot.

Lookalikes: The only Clouded Yellow occurring in Tamil Nadu, immediately separated from lowland grass yellows by its thick spotted borders and montane habitat.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Montane grasslands, shola margins, and temperate hill gardens above 1,500 m.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Restricted to high-elevation Nilgiris (Ooty, Avalanche, Upper Bhavani) and Palani Hills (Kodaikanal).

Seasonality: Year-round in high hills, brightest between October and May.

Flight & behaviour

Flies briskly close to cold montane grassland turf, basking sideways with wings closed to absorb sun heat.

Field note: A classic temperate relic restricted to high shola-grassland plateaus of the Nilgiris and Palani Hills.

Caterpillar host plants
