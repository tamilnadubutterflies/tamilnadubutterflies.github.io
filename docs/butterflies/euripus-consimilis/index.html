Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Apaturinae (Emperors, Princes & Courtesans)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Apaturinae): Rapid-flying forest species with sexual dimorphism and mimicry (such as Painted Courtesan mimicking tigers).

Typical habitats: Mid- to high-elevation evergreen and moist deciduous hill forests.

Field photography tip: Watch wet forest trails and mud patches where males descend briefly to puddle. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.
