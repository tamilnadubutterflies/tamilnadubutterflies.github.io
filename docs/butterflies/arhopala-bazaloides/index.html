Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Theclinae (Silverlines, Oakblues, Royals, Flashes & Yamfly)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Theclinae): Often tailed hairstreaks with metallic silver bars (Cigaritis), deep violet-blue upperwings (Arhopala), or swift red-and-slate flashes (Rapala).

Typical habitats: Scrub jungles, forest canopies, mistletoe-bearing trees, and rocky hilltops.

Field photography tip: Capture the hindwing tornal lobes, tail count, and arrangement of underside bands. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are deep lustrous purplish-blue; females are dark brown with purple wing bases.

Underwing: Pale lilac-brown marked with darker brown outlined bands and dusted with pale frosted scales toward the forewing apex.

Lookalikes: Frosty pale dusting near the forewing apex underside helps distinguish Tamil Oakblue from other brown-banded Arhopala oakblues.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist evergreen and semi-evergreen foothill forests.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Listed among the 4 checklist species awaiting validated TNBS sighting confirmation in Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Mainly post-monsoon and winter.

Flight & behaviour

Skulks in shaded understorey and settles with wings closed.

Field note: Look closely at frosted forewing apices beneath high evergreen canopies; dated photos help validate its Tamil Nadu status.

Caterpillar host plants

Dipterocarpaceae / Fagaceae forest trees (unconfirmed locally)
