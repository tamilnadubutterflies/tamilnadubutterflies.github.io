Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Danainae (Tigers, Crows & Tree Nymphs)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Danainae): Tough, unpalatable butterflies whose caterpillars feed on milkweeds and dogbanes; central to Tamil Nadu’s seasonal Eastern–Western Ghats migrations.

Typical habitats: Hills, plains, urban parks, and forest corridors throughout Tamil Nadu.

Field photography tip: Count subapical spots and pale streaks in the forewing cell to separate Blue Tiger vs Dark Blue Tiger and the Crow species. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Velvety dark-brown crow butterfly common in cities and migration flights

Identification marks

Upperwing: Rich glossy dark brown bordered by two neat submarginal and marginal rows of clean white spots across both wings.

Underwing: Warm dark brown with clear white discal spots in addition to the twin marginal spot rows; males show a single short brand on the forewing.

Lookalikes: Separated from Double-branded Crow (Euploea sylvester) by having one (rather than two) parallel forewing scent brands in males and simpler white inner-forewing spotting.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Urban gardens, avenues, scrub, hill forests, and seasonal migratory flyways.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Statewide across plains, towns, and hills.

Seasonality: Abundant year-round; participates prominently in TNBS pre- and post-monsoon migration flights.

Flight & behaviour

Slow gliding flight; males often visit dry Crotalaria or Heliotropium stems.

Field note: Mimicked by the Common Mime (Papilio clytia) and the female Great Eggfly (Hypolimnas bolina).
