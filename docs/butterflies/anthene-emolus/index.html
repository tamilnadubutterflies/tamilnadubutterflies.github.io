Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

Tailless forest blue whose caterpillars live under weaver-ant protection

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are uniform deep violet-purple with slender dark margins and rounded forewing tips; females are dull brown with faint dull blue basal scales and indistinct dark hindwing marginal dots.

Underwing: Pale grey-brown crossed by regular, unbroken white-edged dark discal bands without a sub-basal spot in space 7 near the hindwing base; bears an orange-capped black tornal spot in space 2 and three tiny hair-like marginal ciliary tufts instead of a true tail.

Lookalikes: Separated from Pointed Ciliate Blue (Anthene lycaenina) by its rounded forewing apex, unbroken forewing discal band, and absence of the dark white-ringed spot near the base of hindwing space 7.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Low- to mid-elevation moist evergreen and semi-evergreen forests, wooded stream edges, and dense canopy clearings.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Restricted and scarce in moist southern and western Tamil Nadu hill forests, notably Kanyakumari Wildlife Sanctuary (Kizhviyar), Kalakkad-Mundanthurai, Siruvani, and Anamalais.

Seasonality: Recorded mainly during pre-monsoon and post-monsoon forest surveys from April to November.

Flight & behaviour

Males fly swiftly around tree canopies and puddle along shaded forest streams; females inspect trees colonized by weaver ants before laying eggs on tender flush.

Field note: Obligate myrmecophile closely tended by Asian Weaver Ants (Oecophylla smaragdina); Pattampoochi Issue 26 documented the May 2023 Kizhviyar record in Kanyakumari WLS as a landmark Tamil Nadu photographic confirmation.
