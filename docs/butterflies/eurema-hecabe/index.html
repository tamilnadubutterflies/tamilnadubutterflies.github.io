Field guide notes for Pieridae · Coliadinae (Yellows, Emigrants & Clouded Yellows)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Coliadinae): Yellow, pale green, or orange butterflies with rapid sustained flight and strong migratory behaviour in several species.

Typical habitats: Open grasslands, agricultural margins, scrub, riverbeds, and Nilgiri/Palani high-altitude meadows (Colias).

Field photography tip: For Eurema grass yellows, zoom into the underside forewing cell to count the small dark cell spots. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

Abundant yellow pierid famous for mass movements in Mudumalai

Identification marks

Upperwing: Bright sulphur-yellow with deep black forewing borders sharply indented in the middle like the silhouette of an inverted '3'.

Underwing: Yellow speckled with variable brown smudges and exactly two small black spots inside the forewing cell.

Lookalikes: Separated from Three-spot Grass Yellow (Eurema blanda) by having 2 (rather than 3) spots inside the underside forewing cell and a cleaner forewing tip.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Grasslands, roadsides, gardens, agricultural fields, scrub, and forest edges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Found across every habitat and elevation in Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Year-round statewide; thousands migrated together during the June 2024 Mudumalai survey.

Flight & behaviour

Weak fluttering flight close to the ground; gathers in bright yellow puddles on moist soil.

Field note: During the June 2024 Mudumalai Tiger Reserve Monsoon Survey, TNBS recorded thousands of Common Grass Yellows moving together, a notable mass-movement event.
