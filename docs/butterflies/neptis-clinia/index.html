Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Limenitidinae (Sailers, Sergeants, Lascars, Barons & Clipper)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Limenitidinae): Gliding butterflies with characteristic black-and-white banded wings (Neptis, Athyma) or emerald-olive camouflaged upperwings (Euthalia, Dophla).

Typical habitats: Forest trails, stream banks, orchards, and wooded gardens.

Field photography tip: Check whether the forewing cell streak is broken into spots or continuous, and compare underside ground colour. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

Rare evergreen-forest sailer with rufous underwings and tight cell spots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark black-brown with narrow, slightly dusky white markings; the forewing cell streak and the triangular spot just beyond it sit closely together or nearly touch, and the hindwing white discal band is broken into well-separated spots along the veins.

Underwing: Deep warm rufous-brown to dark ferruginous with clean white bands lacking black outlines, the forewing cell streak and adjacent spot often conjoined beneath.

Lookalikes: Closely resembles Common Sailer (Neptis hylas) and Clear Sailer (Neptis nata hampsoni), but lacks N. hylas's crisp black-outlined golden-ochre underside and shows darker ferruginous underwings with narrowly separated hindwing discal spots.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Shaded wet evergreen and semi-evergreen hill forests, stream ravines, and dense canopy openings.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Rare Western Ghats forest specialist in Tamil Nadu, recorded from Nilgiris, Anamalai, and Tirunelveli-Kanyakumari wet hill tracts.

Seasonality: Scarce and localized, recorded mainly from September to January and during light summer showers.

Flight & behaviour

Flies and glides low along shaded forest trails and stream gullies, resting briefly with outstretched wings on damp rocks and understory shrubs.

Field note: Represented in southern India by the endemic Sahyadri race Neptis clinia kallaura; careful inspection of the forewing cell streak and underside vein spacing separates it from N. nata.
