Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

Sharp-tipped forest lineblue with a yellow-orange ring at the tornus

Identification marks

Upperwing: Forewing apex distinctly acute and outer margin straight; males are deep dark violet-blue with a slender black border, whereas females are dark brown with brilliant metallic blue restricted strictly to the wing bases.

Underwing: Pale greyish-brown marked with fine, broken white transverse lines enclosing slightly darker bands, a prominent black subtornal spot in space 2 encircled by a bright yellow-orange ring, and a delicate white-tipped tail.

Lookalikes: Readily told from Common Lineblue (Prosotas nora) and Nacaduba species by its sharply pointed forewing silhouette, restricted basal blue on the female upperwing, and bright yellow-orange ring around the tornal eyespot.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Evergreen and moist semi-evergreen forest understories, shaded nullahs, and bamboo valleys.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Occurs in humid Western and Eastern Ghats forests of Tamil Nadu including Siruvani, Kallar, Mudumalai, Anamalai, Kalakkad-Mundanthurai, Kanyakumari, and Shevaroys.

Seasonality: Active throughout moist months, peaking from August to February.

Flight & behaviour

Keeps to shaded forest clearings and stream edges, fluttering swiftly around Allophylus shrubs and puddling singly or in small numbers on wet rocks.

Field note: In contrast to legume-feeding Prosotas lineblues, Pointed Lineblue caterpillars specialize on tender shoots and flower buds of Allophylus cobbe.

Caterpillar host plants

Allophylus cobbe (Sapindaceae)
