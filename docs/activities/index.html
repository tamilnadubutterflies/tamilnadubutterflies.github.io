Activities and fieldwork

From Mudumalai and Pachamalai Forest Division butterfly surveys to school outreach, City Nature Challenge walks, and statewide migration tracking.

Surveys, walks & outreach

Surveys · December 2024

Pachamalai Hills Butterfly Survey

Conducted with Tiruchirappalli Forest Division across 7 reserve forests and eco-tourism zones, documenting 126 butterfly species across all 6 families, including the Schedule II Banded Royal, bringing Pachamalai's combined checklist to 149.

Open field report

Surveys · June 2024

Mudumalai Tiger Reserve Monsoon Survey

Coordinated with Tamil Nadu Forest Department and WWF-India across 16 teams in Udhagai and Masinagudi divisions, recording 185+ butterfly species and observing thousands of migrating Common Grass Yellows.

Read the coverage

Outreach · November 2024

TNBS Government School Tamil Poster Project

Free Tamil butterfly awareness posters and educational booklets distributed across government schools in 13+ Tamil Nadu districts including Chennai, Salem, Krishnagiri, Tiruppur, Dharmapuri, Cuddalore, and The Nilgiris.

Open field report

Butterfly walks · November 2024

Coimbatore Vizha Walk at Kovai Kutralam

Joint walk by TNBS and Coimbatore Nature Society introducing 20 newcomers to butterfly watching, recording 71+ butterfly species including Clipper, Indian Tawny Rajah, Tamil Yeoman, and Short-banded Sailer.

Open field report

Citizen science · April 2024 & 2025

City Nature Challenge, Coimbatore & Singanallur

TNBS led butterfly walks at Singanallur and joined Coimbatore conservation groups to help document 1,820+ taxa across urban wetlands and parks.

Read the coverage

Surveys · January & March 2024

Coimbatore Forest Division Synchronised Survey

TNBS teamed up with the Tamil Nadu Forest Department, Coimbatore Nature Society, and WWF-India across wetland and terrestrial survey phases.

Read the coverage

Outreach · January 2024

School Awareness Sessions & Kallar Walk

Led a Kovai Vizha butterfly walk at Kallar (56 species recorded) and addressed students and educators from Coimbatore and Pollachi districts on butterflies and host plants.

Open field report

Monitoring · 2013 to Present

Tamil Nadu Danainae Migration Monitoring

Over a decade tracking pre-monsoon (April-May) and post-monsoon (September-November) flights of Blue Tigers, Dark Blue Tigers, and Crows between the Eastern and Western Ghats.
