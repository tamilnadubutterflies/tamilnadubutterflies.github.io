Who we are

About us

The Nature and Butterfly Society (TNBS) is a registered Coimbatore-based citizen-science society (Reg. No. 259/2018 (Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act, 1975)) uniting naturalists, researchers, students, and forest partners to document butterflies across Tamil Nadu.

What began in 2012-2014 as an effort to build Tamil Nadu's first dedicated state checklist has grown into a thriving community of Tamilnadu Butterflies Facebook group (founded 02 Oct 2014; 2,340+ members across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu & Puducherry).

From joint monsoon surveys in Mudumalai Tiger Reserve and Pachamalai Hills to tracking cross-state tiger and crow migrations and distributing free Tamil butterfly posters to government schools, TNBS turns careful field observations into open conservation knowledge.

Read our full story & teamExplore fieldwork & surveys
  • 2012 - 2014Base checklist compilation begins; Tamilnadu Butterflies Facebook group launched (02 Oct 2014) to gather verified sightings across districts.
  • Jan 2015 - 2017Tamil Nadu Butterfly Checklist v1 prepared (323 species; 271 validated initially) and quarterly e-magazine Pattampoochi launched in Q1 2017.
  • 2018 - 2019Registered as The Nature and Butterfly Society (259/2018); state butterfly discussions and steady expansion of verified checklist records to 325 species.
  • 2021 - 2023Siruvani Hills documented as a butterfly super-hotspot; joint Tamil Nadu Forest Department surveys across Mudumalai, Sathyamangalam, Coimbatore, Erode, Salem, and Sirumalai.
