Pattampoochi Issue 27 Adopted Final Checklist

This website adopts the Pattampoochi Issue 27 (January-March 2025) Tamil Nadu Butterfly Checklist update by A. Pavendhan as final: 329 species across 6 families, with 325 validated species and 4 target species awaiting validated photographic records in Tamil Nadu.

Latest Issue 27 checklist additions highlighted in the field guide: Common Palmfly (Elymnias hypermnestra) and Cloud Forest Silverline (Cigaritis meghamalaiensis, Naicker et al. 2023).

Four checklist species awaiting validated Tamil Nadu confirmation

  • Creamy Sailer (Neptis palnica)
  • Tamil Oakblue (Arhopala bazaloides)
  • Common Awl (Hasora badra)
  • Coorg Forest Hopper (Arnetta mercara)
