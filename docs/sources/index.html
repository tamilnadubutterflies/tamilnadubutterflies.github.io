Tamil Nadu Butterfly Checklist

This website presents TNBS's 329-species Tamil Nadu Butterfly Checklist across 6 families (compiled and updated through Pattampoochi, with 325 validated species and 4 priority species awaiting validated photographic records in Tamil Nadu).

Recent state additions highlighted in the field guide include Common Palmfly (Elymnias hypermnestra) and Cloud Forest Silverline (Cigaritis meghamalaiensis, Naicker et al. 2023).

Four checklist species awaiting validated Tamil Nadu confirmation

  • Creamy Sailer (Neptis palnica)
  • Tamil Oakblue (Arhopala bazaloides)
  • Common Awl (Hasora badra)
  • Coorg Forest Hopper (Arnetta mercara)
