How it works

Tips for high-accuracy AI & expert ID

  • Fill the frame cleanly: Crop close around the butterfly so wing spots, bands, and margins stand out clearly.
  • Capture both surfaces when possible: Underwing patterns are vital for grass blues, bushbrowns, silverlines, and skippers.
  • Use Western Ghats vs All India strategically: For Nilgiris, Anamalai, or KMTR photos, try Western Ghats first; switch to All India for coastal plains or Eastern Ghats.
  • Cross-verify in the TNBS Field Guide: Confirm seasonal morphs, distribution maps, and host plants on the species page.
