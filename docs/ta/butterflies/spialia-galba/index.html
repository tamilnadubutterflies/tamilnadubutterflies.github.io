Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் நரைச்சிறகுத் தாவி. Indian Skipper (Spialia galba) என்பது தாவிகள் (விரிசிறகுத் தாவிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 394 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Tiny checkered black-and-white skipper basking flat on sunlit ground

Identification marks

Upperwing: Charcoal-black checkered neatly with crisp white spots across both forewings and hindwings.

Underwing: Olive-grey crossed by clean broken white bands.

Lookalikes: Smaller and evenly checkered with black-and-white bars across the margins compared to African Marbled Skipper (Gomalia elma).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Sunny lawns, dry scrub, rocky paths, fallow fields, and open plains.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Common across Tamil Nadu plains and open foothills.

Seasonality: Year-round in sunny habitats.

Flight & behaviour

Darts rapidly inches above sunlit ground and perches with wings wide open.

Field note: Tucked neatly under dry grass heads at dusk, resembling a small checkered moth until sunlight hits its spread wings.
