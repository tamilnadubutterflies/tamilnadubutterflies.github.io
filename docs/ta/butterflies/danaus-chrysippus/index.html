Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Danainae (Tigers, Crows & Tree Nymphs)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Danainae): Tough, unpalatable butterflies whose caterpillars feed on milkweeds and dogbanes; central to Tamil Nadu’s seasonal Eastern–Western Ghats migrations.

Typical habitats: Hills, plains, urban parks, and forest corridors throughout Tamil Nadu.

Field photography tip: Count subapical spots and pale streaks in the forewing cell to separate Blue Tiger vs Dark Blue Tiger and the Crow species. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் வெந்தய வரியன். Plain Tiger (Danaus chrysippus) என்பது வரியன்கள் (நீல வரியன்கள் மற்றும் கருப்பன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 3,353 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Iconic orange-and-black monarch butterfly of open plains and gardens

Identification marks

Upperwing: Warm tawny-orange with jet-black forewing tips slashed by a crisp row of white spots and a black-spotted hindwing centre.

Underwing: Paler golden-orange with matching black borders and bright white spots.

Lookalikes: Unmarked tawny veins distinguish it from the boldly black-veined Striped Tiger (Danaus genutia) and the female Danaid Eggfly mimic (which lacks small black central hindwing spots).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Gardens, roadside milkweeds, dry plains, coastal dunes, and open hills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Found in virtually all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Abundant year-round across Tamil Nadu.

Flight & behaviour

Slow, buoyant flight; frequently visits Tridax, Lantana, and Calotropis blooms.

Field note: Serves as the model mimicked by females of the Danaid Eggfly (Hypolimnas misippus).
