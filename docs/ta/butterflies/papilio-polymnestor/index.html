Field guide notes for Papilionidae · Papilioninae (True Swallowtails & Birdwings)

Family overview (Papilionidae, Swallowtails): Large to very large, strong-flying butterflies including birdwings, roses, mimes, Mormons, jays, swordtails, and banded peacocks. Many species carry prominent hindwing tails and feed actively while fluttering their wings at flowers.

Subfamily traits (Papilioninae): Includes Graphium jays and swordtails, Pachliopta roses, Papilio swallowtails, and the Southern Birdwing (Troides minos).

Typical habitats: Forest edges, hill streams, gardens, scrub, and evergreen shola margins across Tamil Nadu.

Field photography tip: Note hindwing tail shape, body colour (red vs black), and submarginal spot patterns. Both upperwing (dorsal) and underwing (ventral) photographs help separate mimics, mimes, and closely related dark Papilio species.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் நீல அழகி. Blue Mormon (Papilio polymnestor) என்பது அழகிகள் (அழகிகள் மற்றும் பறவையிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும்.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Tailless giant swallowtail with powdery pale blue hindwings

Identification marks

Upperwing: Velvety black forewings streaked with pale blue post-discal bands and large pale sky-blue outer hindwings dotted with two rows of black spots.

Underwing: Deep black with faint red basal patches and wide greyish-white outer hindwing area bearing black spots.

Lookalikes: Unmistakable in southern India, tailless, huge, and bright sky-blue on the outer half of the hindwing.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Wooded gardens, citrus orchards, moist deciduous foothills, and evergreen forests.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Common in the Western and Eastern Ghats (Mudumalai, Siruvani, Kallar, Pachamalai) and frequent in wooded plains and cities.

Seasonality: Year-round, with highest counts during monsoon and post-monsoon months.

Flight & behaviour

Glides powerfully through forest glades and feeds at Ixora, Mussaenda, and Lantana without settling still for long.

Field note: TNBS checklist retains Papilio polymnestor for the Blue Mormon; Butterflies of India treats the taxon in the Papilio memnon/agenor complex.
