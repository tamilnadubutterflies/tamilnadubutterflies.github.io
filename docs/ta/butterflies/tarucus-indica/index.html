Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Pointed Pierrot (Tarucus indica) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 12 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Slightly sharp-winged translucent pierrot of dry thorn scrub

Identification marks

Upperwing: Forewing apex slightly more pointed than Tarucus nara; males are pale, semi-translucent violet-blue showing faint shadows of underside spots through the membrane, with a thin border and cell-end dash; females are brown with white discal blotches.

Underwing: Crisp white marked with sharp, well-spaced black dots, an oblique broken postdiscal series on the forewing, a broken submarginal row of neat round spots, subtle metallic green scaling on hindwing tornal spots, and a slender tail.

Lookalikes: Separated from Rounded Pierrot (Tarucus nara) by its slightly pointed forewing, translucent upperwing, and broken submarginal spots; differs from Spotted Pierrot (Tarucus callinara) reliably by male valva shape.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Semi-arid scrub jungles, thorny Ziziphus hedgerows, dry riverbeds, and open plains.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Occurs across dry interior plains and foothills of Tamil Nadu including Coimbatore, Erode, Dharmapuri, Salem, Madurai, and Tirunelveli.

Seasonality: Recorded predominantly in dry and post-monsoon seasons from October to April.

Flight & behaviour

Flits nervously around thorny shrubs in open sun and settles on dry stems or small nectar flowers close to the ground.

Field note: Originally treated as an Indian race of Tarucus theophrastus; caterpillars graze soft epidermis from Ziziphus leaves while attended by Crematogaster ants.
