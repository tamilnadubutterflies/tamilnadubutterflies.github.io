Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Black-spotted Pierrot (Tarucus balkanicus) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும்.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Rare dark-spotted thorn-scrub pierrot recorded in Coimbatore wetlands

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are glossy dark violet-blue (often suffused dusky smoky-brown in Indian subspecies nigra) with a bold black cell-end spot and distinct black discal spots in forewing spaces 1b to 4; females are dark brown mottled with pale bluish-white central spots.

Underwing: Chalk-white heavily marked in jet-black: a solid streak along the forewing subcostal-cell zone, an oblique black discal bar below vein 4, separated upper postdiscal spots, broken submarginal dots, metallic green-flecked tornal spots, and a fine tail.

Lookalikes: Separated from Rounded Pierrot (Tarucus nara) by clear black postdiscal spots on the male upperwing and broken submarginal dots beneath rather than a continuous black submarginal line.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Arid and semi-arid thorn scrub, wetland bunds, and dry Ziziphus thickets.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Rare in Tamil Nadu with verified dry-zone and wetland records from Coimbatore (documented in Pattampoochi Issue 5), Erode, Sathyamangalam, and rainshadow plains.

Seasonality: Scarce and seasonal, chiefly noted during dry and post-monsoon periods from October to March.

Flight & behaviour

Flies briskly around low thorny Ziziphus bushes in harsh midday heat and pauses briefly on dry twigs or damp mud edges.

Field note: Indian subspecies Tarucus balkanicus nigra was spotlighted in Pattampoochi Issue 5 (Jan-Mar 2018) as a rare Tamil Nadu find; larvae are tended by ants on Ziziphus.
