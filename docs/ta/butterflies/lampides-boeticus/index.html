Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் பட்டாணி நீலன். Pea Blue (Lampides boeticus) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 321 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Long-distance migratory blue tailed with twin black eyespots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are soft frosted violet-blue; females are brown with blue wing bases; both carry two conspicuous black dots near the delicate hindwing tails.

Underwing: Pale buff-brown neatly rippled with white wavy stripes, a crisp white submarginal band on the hindwing, and twin orange-crowned black eyespots.

Lookalikes: Rippled brown-and-white underside with a clean pale hindwing band separates it from Zebra Blue (which has stronger zebra-stripe contrast) and Ciliate Blues (which lack tails).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Gardens, agricultural fields, fallow scrub, open hills, and Crotalaria patches.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Statewide from coastal plains to Nilgiri peaks.

Seasonality: Common year-round, especially abundant October–March.

Flight & behaviour

Brisk erratic flight around flowering legumes; famous global migrant.

Field note: Cosmopolitan legume-pod feeder whose larvae develop inside rattlepod (Crotalaria) and cultivated bean pods.
