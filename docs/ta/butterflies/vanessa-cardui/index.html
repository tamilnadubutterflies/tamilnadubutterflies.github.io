Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Nymphalinae (Pansies, Eggflies, Admirals & Oakleaf)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Nymphalinae): Familiar, colourful butterflies featuring conspicuous eyespots, seasonal morphs, and dramatic dead-leaf underside camouflage.

Typical habitats: Gardens, wetlands, agricultural bunds, scrublands, and hill forests.

Field photography tip: In Junonia pansies and Hypolimnas eggflies, both dorsal colour patches and ventral patterns aid rapid ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Painted Lady (Vanessa cardui) என்பது வரியன்கள் (வசீகரன்கள் மற்றும் மாய வரியன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 120 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Cosmopolitan migrant recorded across hills and interior plains

Identification marks

Upperwing: Warm pinkish-orange wings patterned with black blotches and dark forewing tips dotted with bright white spots.

Underwing: Intricate marbled buff, rose-pink, and olive pattern bearing four neat submarginal eyespots on the hindwing.

Lookalikes: Lacks the bright crimson-red forewing band of the Indian Red Admiral (Vanessa indica).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Open grasslands, hills, dry scrub, fallow farmland, and urban gardens.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Recorded from high Nilgiri/Palani peaks down to Sivaganga, Coimbatore, and coastal plains.

Seasonality: Irregular year-round; notable invasions documented in Pattampoochi (Q4 2019) and June 2026 Sivaganga press coverage.

Flight & behaviour

Swift erratic flyer known for eruptive long-distance movements across Tamil Nadu.

Field note: Holds one of the longest known insect migration circuits globally, reaching Tamil Nadu hills and plains irregularly in winter.
