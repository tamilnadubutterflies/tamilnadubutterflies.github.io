Field guide notes for Papilionidae · Papilioninae (True Swallowtails & Birdwings)

Family overview (Papilionidae, Swallowtails): Large to very large, strong-flying butterflies including birdwings, roses, mimes, Mormons, jays, swordtails, and banded peacocks. Many species carry prominent hindwing tails and feed actively while fluttering their wings at flowers.

Subfamily traits (Papilioninae): Includes Graphium jays and swordtails, Pachliopta roses, Papilio swallowtails, and the Southern Birdwing (Troides minos).

Typical habitats: Forest edges, hill streams, gardens, scrub, and evergreen shola margins across Tamil Nadu.

Field photography tip: Note hindwing tail shape, body colour (red vs black), and submarginal spot patterns. Both upperwing (dorsal) and underwing (ventral) photographs help separate mimics, mimes, and closely related dark Papilio species.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் சிவப்புடல் அழகி. Crimson Rose (Pachliopta hector) என்பது அழகிகள் (அழகிகள் மற்றும் பறவையிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 1,187 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

A bold red-bodied swallowtail common across plains and hills

Identification marks

Upperwing: Jet-black forewings crossed by two broken white bands and hindwings bearing two bright rows of crimson-red crescent spots; head and abdomen are bright crimson.

Underwing: Matches the crisp black, white-banded, and crimson-spotted dorsal pattern; red body acts as a warning signal of Aristolochia-derived toxins.

Lookalikes: Separated from the Common Rose (Pachliopta aristolochiae) by the two distinct white bands across the black forewing and vivid crimson hindwing spots.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Open scrublands, coastal plains, urban gardens, dry deciduous foothills, and cultivated landscapes.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Statewide from Chennai coast and interior plains to Western and Eastern Ghats foothills.

Seasonality: Common throughout the year across Tamil Nadu.

Flight & behaviour

Flies slowly with effortless wingbeats just above vegetation and participates in local coastal and peninsular movements.

Field note: Acts as the distasteful Batesian model mimicked by the female Common Mormon (hector form).
