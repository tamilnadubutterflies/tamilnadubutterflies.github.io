Field guide notes for Pieridae · Coliadinae (Yellows, Emigrants & Clouded Yellows)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Coliadinae): Yellow, pale green, or orange butterflies with rapid sustained flight and strong migratory behaviour in several species.

Typical habitats: Open grasslands, agricultural margins, scrub, riverbeds, and Nilgiri/Palani high-altitude meadows (Colias).

Field photography tip: For Eurema grass yellows, zoom into the underside forewing cell to count the small dark cell spots. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் அவரை வெள்ளையன். Mottled Emigrant (Catopsilia pyranthe) என்பது வெள்ளையன்கள் (புல் மஞ்சள் மற்றும் கொன்னை வெள்ளையன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 1,405 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Pale greenish-white migrant pierced by tiny black forewing cell dots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Chalky greenish-white with narrow black outer margins on the forewing and a small black oval dot at the end of the forewing cell; females carry heavier borders and spotting.

Underwing: Densely etched in fine greenish-brown wavy striae ('mottled') over a pale creamy-green ground with tiny silver-ringed pink-edged cell dots.

Lookalikes: Slightly smaller than Common Emigrant (Catopsilia pomona), showing a prominent dorsal black forewing cell spot and finely mottled underside hatching.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Urban lawns, roadside Senna patches, scrub plains, wetlands, and agricultural hedges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Occurs in all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu, thriving wherever roadside Senna weeds grow.

Seasonality: Abundant year-round across Tamil Nadu plains and foothills.

Flight & behaviour

Fast, bouncing daytime flyer that pauses frequently at bright red/pink flowers and forms mixed directional flights ahead of monsoons.

Field note: Easily studied in city gardens where females oviposit rapidly on young Senna occidentalis buds.
