Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Nymphalinae (Pansies, Eggflies, Admirals & Oakleaf)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Nymphalinae): Familiar, colourful butterflies featuring conspicuous eyespots, seasonal morphs, and dramatic dead-leaf underside camouflage.

Typical habitats: Gardens, wetlands, agricultural bunds, scrublands, and hill forests.

Field photography tip: In Junonia pansies and Hypolimnas eggflies, both dorsal colour patches and ventral patterns aid rapid ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் எலுமிச்சை வண்ணன். Lemon Pansy (Junonia lemonias) என்பது வரியன்கள் (வசீகரன்கள் மற்றும் மாய வரியன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 2,086 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Olive-brown garden pansy spotted in pale lemon with bright eyespots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Olive-brown dotted with creamy lemon-yellow spots and bright blue-centered orange-ringed eyespots.

Underwing: Warm ochre-brown to dark reddish leaf-brown in dry-season forms.

Lookalikes: Lemon-yellow forewing spots separate it from the uniformly dark Chocolate Pansy and grey-white Grey Pansy.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Gardens, roadside weeds, agricultural edges, scrub, and forest trails.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Occurs statewide from urban gardens to hills.

Seasonality: Very common all year across Tamil Nadu.

Flight & behaviour

Low, brisk territorial flight; perches frequently on bare ground or low foliage with wings fully open.

Field note: Uses its cryptic brownish underside to blend seamlessly onto gravelly soil while basking flat in morning sun.
