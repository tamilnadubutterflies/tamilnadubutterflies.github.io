Field guide notes for Papilionidae · Papilioninae (True Swallowtails & Birdwings)

Family overview (Papilionidae, Swallowtails): Large to very large, strong-flying butterflies including birdwings, roses, mimes, Mormons, jays, swordtails, and banded peacocks. Many species carry prominent hindwing tails and feed actively while fluttering their wings at flowers.

Subfamily traits (Papilioninae): Includes Graphium jays and swordtails, Pachliopta roses, Papilio swallowtails, and the Southern Birdwing (Troides minos).

Typical habitats: Forest edges, hill streams, gardens, scrub, and evergreen shola margins across Tamil Nadu.

Field photography tip: Note hindwing tail shape, body colour (red vs black), and submarginal spot patterns. Both upperwing (dorsal) and underwing (ventral) photographs help separate mimics, mimes, and closely related dark Papilio species.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் கறிவேப்பிலை அழகி. Common Mormon (Papilio polytes) என்பது அழகிகள் (அழகிகள் மற்றும் பறவையிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 1,674 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Classic garden swallowtail famous for female Batesian mimicry

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are black with a neat chain of white spots across the hindwing; females occur in three forms, male-like 'cyrus', Common Rose mimic 'stichius', and Crimson Rose mimic 'romulus'.

Underwing: Dark brown-black with white discal spots and submarginal orange-red or white crescents depending on morph.

Lookalikes: Mimetic females have black bodies instead of the bright crimson body found in true Pachliopta rose swallowtails.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Home gardens, citrus groves, parks, scrub, and forest edges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Found in almost every district of Tamil Nadu from coastal cities to hill valleys.

Seasonality: Abundant year-round statewide.

Flight & behaviour

Active sun-lover weaving briskly through curry-leaf and lemon bushes while fluttering wings at flowers.

Field note: A textbook demonstration of polymorphic Batesian mimicry observable right in Tamil Nadu backyard curry-leaf plants.
