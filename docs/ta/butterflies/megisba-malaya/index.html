Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Malayan (Megisba malaya) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 23 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

White-patched woodland blue sporting four dark spots along the forewing costa

Identification marks

Upperwing: South Indian subspecies thwaitesi is tailless; dark blackish-brown in both sexes with an oblique white central patch across the forewing (often extending onto the hindwing in dry-season broods) and no blue sheen.

Underwing: Dull greyish-white peppered with fine dark lines, bearing a diagnostic row of four small black spots along the forewing costa, three distinct black sub-basal spots across the hindwing base plus a dark cell-end bar, and two black subtornal spots.

Lookalikes: Differs from Quaker (Neopithecops zalmora) by its four neat black spots along the forewing underside costa and three sub-basal spots across the hindwing base; southern race lacks the tail seen in northeastern Megisba populations.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Evergreen, semi-evergreen, and moist deciduous forest edges, shaded streams, and wooded plains.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Found across Western and Eastern Ghats forests and moist wooded foothills of Tamil Nadu including Siruvani, Kallar, Mudumalai, Anamalai, Kalakkad-Mundanthurai, Shevaroys, Kolli Hills, and Pachamalai.

Seasonality: Recorded year-round in wooded hills, peaking between August and February.

Flight & behaviour

Flies weakly in dappled shade along forest paths and gathers readily on damp sand, wet rocks, and bird droppings.

Field note: South Indian and Sri Lankan subspecies Megisba malaya thwaitesi is consistently tailless, making ventral costal-spot inspection essential in the field.
