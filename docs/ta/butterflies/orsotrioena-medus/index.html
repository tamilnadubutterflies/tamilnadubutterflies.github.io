Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Satyrinae (Browns, Bushbrowns, Treebrowns & Rings)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Satyrinae): Shade-loving brown butterflies marked with concentric underside eyespots (ocelli) that frequently exhibit wet- and dry-season forms.

Typical habitats: Grasslands, forest understorey, bamboo groves, palm groves, and shaded gardens.

Field photography tip: Underside ocelli sizes, brand marks, and discal line shape (straight vs curved) are crucial for species ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் மிருதுக் கண் புதர்ப் பழுப்பு. Nigger (Orsotrioena medus) என்பது வரியன்கள் (பழுப்பு மற்றும் நால்வளையன் சிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 36 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Grass-dwelling dark brown marked underneath by a straight white bar and metallic-ringed eyespots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Plain dark velvety brown devoid of upperwing eyespots and edged only by thin pale marginal and submarginal hairlines; males carry a black hair-tufted scent fold near the forewing dorsum.

Underwing: Crossed cleanly across both wings by a straight pure white discal bar (narrower in dry season); wet-season adults show 2 forewing and 3 hindwing ocelli (two apical, one isolated tornal) ringed in gleaming silvery-violet, while dry-season morphs turn pale buff with tiny specks.

Lookalikes: Separated from all Mycalesis bushbrowns by having zero eyespots on the upperwing, a straight stark-white underside band, and exactly 2 forewing plus 3 hindwing ocelli.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Damp grassy clearings, paddy margins, marsh edges, and humid forest trails.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Western Ghats foothills, humid valleys, and wet coastal or agricultural pockets including Gudalur, Mudumalai, Siruvani, Anamalais, Kanyakumari, and irrigated plains.

Seasonality: Active year-round in humid habitats, with strongly ocellated wet-season forms peaking June-December.

Flight & behaviour

Flies slowly just above wet grass blades and settles quickly low in vegetation, visiting damp soil and fermenting fruit rather than flowers.

Field note: Southern Indian populations represent the nominate subspecies O. m. medus and can thrive around wet rice paddies bordering forests.
