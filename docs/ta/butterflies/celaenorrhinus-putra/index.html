Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Bengal Spotted Flat (Celaenorrhinus putra) என்பது தாவிகள் (விரிசிறகுத் தாவிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 2 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Pitch-dark spotted flat with unchequered cilia and an almost unmarked hindwing

Identification marks

Upperwing: Deep sooty blackish-brown with narrower fused white discal spots on the forewing, a separated small spot in space 1b, almost unmarked dark hindwings, and dull brownish-white unchequered cilia.

Underwing: Uniform blackish-brown echoing the clean white forewing spots with scarcely any yellow hindwing mottling; antennal club is white while the shaft remains dark brown in males.

Lookalikes: Darker sooty ground colour, unchequered hindwing cilia, dark male antennal shaft, and nearly plain upper hindwing separate it from Common Spotted Flat (Celaenorrhinus leucocera).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Dense wet evergreen forests, shaded hill streams, and deep understorey ravines.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Restricted to wet Western Ghats forests of Tamil Nadu including Siruvani, Nilgiris, Valparai, Anamalai, Meghamalai, and Kalakkad-Mundanthurai.

Seasonality: Mainly monsoon and post-monsoon months from June to December.

Flight & behaviour

Skulks in dark forest undergrowth, resting flat beneath broad leaves or damp rocks and briefly visiting blossoms in dappled sunbeams.

Field note: Subspecies Celaenorrhinus putra sanda occurs in southern India and prefers deeper, damper evergreen shade than C. leucocera.
