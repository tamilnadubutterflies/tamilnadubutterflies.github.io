Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Satyrinae (Browns, Bushbrowns, Treebrowns & Rings)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Satyrinae): Shade-loving brown butterflies marked with concentric underside eyespots (ocelli) that frequently exhibit wet- and dry-season forms.

Typical habitats: Grasslands, forest understorey, bamboo groves, palm groves, and shaded gardens.

Field photography tip: Underside ocelli sizes, brand marks, and discal line shape (straight vs curved) are crucial for species ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Small Long-brand Bushbrown (Mycalesis igilia) என்பது வரியன்கள் (பழுப்பு மற்றும் நால்வளையன் சிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும்.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Compact Western Ghats endemic bushbrown with a slender pale-brown forewing brand

Identification marks

Upperwing: Smaller and slightly rounder-winged than M. visala; dark greyish-brown bearing a distinct yellow-ringed black ocellus in space 2 of the forewing and 1-2 faint small ocelli on the hindwing.

Underwing: Paler warm brown crossed by a slender whitish-violet discal fascia and a complete submarginal arc of ringed ocelli (2-4 on forewing, 7 on hindwing); males bear a narrow, moderately long pale ochre-brown scent brand in space 1b alongside a small hindwing costal brand.

Lookalikes: Smaller wingspan, rounder forewing apex, and narrower pale brown male brand separate it from Long-brand Bushbrown (Mycalesis visala), while its longer brand separates it from Dark-brand Bushbrown (Mycalesis mineus).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Shaded grassy understorey of moist deciduous and semi-evergreen Western Ghats foothills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Western Ghats foothills and slopes of Tamil Nadu including Mudumalai, Gudalur, Siruvani, Anamalais, and Kalakkad-Mundanthurai.

Seasonality: Most frequently recorded between July and January following monsoon grass growth.

Flight & behaviour

Shade-tolerant low flier that stays close to damp forest trails and tangled grass clumps, visiting fermenting fruit and moist soil.

Field note: Endemic to southern India and often overlooked among lookalike Mycalesis species unless the male forewing brand and wing shape are examined closely.
