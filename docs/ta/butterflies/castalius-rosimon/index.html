Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் விகடன். Common Pierrot (Castalius rosimon) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 1,279 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Dainty white-and-black spotted pierrot common in gardens and scrub

Identification marks

Upperwing: White wings with metallic pale-blue iridescent bases, dark margins, and bold scattered black spots.

Underwing: Pure white neatly spotted and streaked with jet-black marks and a delicate hair-like tail.

Lookalikes: Differs from Angled Pierrot (Caleta decidia) and Banded Blue Pierrot (Discolampa ethion) by discrete scattered underside spots rather than solid dark diagonal bands.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Scrublands, roadside Ziziphus shrubs, gardens, wetlands, and dry foothills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Common across plains, cities, and hills of Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Year-round statewide.

Flight & behaviour

Weak fluttering flight close to thorny jujube bushes; mud-puddles at damp patches.

Field note: Flutters just above Ziziphus jujube bushes; caterpillars graze characteristic translucent windows on young leaves.
