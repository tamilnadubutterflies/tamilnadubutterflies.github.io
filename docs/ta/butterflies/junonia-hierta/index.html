Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Nymphalinae (Pansies, Eggflies, Admirals & Oakleaf)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Nymphalinae): Familiar, colourful butterflies featuring conspicuous eyespots, seasonal morphs, and dramatic dead-leaf underside camouflage.

Typical habitats: Gardens, wetlands, agricultural bunds, scrublands, and hill forests.

Field photography tip: In Junonia pansies and Hypolimnas eggflies, both dorsal colour patches and ventral patterns aid rapid ID. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் மஞ்சள் வண்ணன். Yellow Pansy (Junonia hierta) என்பது வரியன்கள் (வசீகரன்கள் மற்றும் மாய வரியன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 358 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Sun-loving lemon-yellow pansy stamped with an iridescent blue hindwing patch

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are brilliant lemon-yellow framed by a jet-black forewing costa and apex holding a pale yellow bar, plus a black hindwing costal patch enclosing a bright metallic sky-blue spot; females are duller ochre-yellow with broader dark borders and twin small ringed eyespots.

Underwing: Pale sandy-buff to greyish-yellow etched with fine dusky striations and faint eyespots, turning warmer ochre-brown with dead-grass camouflage in dry-season morphs.

Lookalikes: Bright lemon-yellow colouration and black-and-blue hindwing patch separate it cleanly from Blue Pansy (Junonia orithya) and orange Peacock Pansy (Junonia almana).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Dry open scrublands, fallow fields, sunny lawns, sandy riverbeds, and bare rocky hill slopes.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread statewide from Chennai coastal scrub and dry southern plains (Madurai, Ramanathapuram, Tirunelveli) to Coimbatore and open hill slopes.

Seasonality: Common year-round across sunny habitats, peaking in dry bright months from November to May.

Flight & behaviour

Low, brisk territorial flier that basks with flatly open wings on warm bare earth, rocks, and Tridax flowers, darting out at passing insects.

Field note: Males show stark black-yellow-blue contrast to signal territory holders, while females rely on duller patterns and eyespots when ovipositing near ground level.
