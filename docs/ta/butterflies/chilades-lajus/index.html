Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் எலுமிச்சை நீலன். Lime Blue (Chilades lajus) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 969 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Tailless citrus blue showing a dark hindwing blotch in dry winter broods

Identification marks

Upperwing: Tailless; males are deep purple-blue with a slender dark marginal border and dusky hindwing marginal spots, while females are dark brown with vivid metallic blue confined to the basal half of both wings and pale-ringed dark hindwing spots.

Underwing: Dull brownish-grey densely speckled with white-ringed black spots and bars (developing a large diffuse dark chocolate cloud across the hindwing disc in dry-season forms), small dark subtornal dots flecked faintly with metallic scales, and no orange crowns.

Lookalikes: Separated from Plains Cupid (Chilades pandava) and Gram Blue (Euchrysops cnejus) by lacking tails and orange tornal crowns, and from Grass Blues by its larger size, dense dark spots, and dry-season hindwing blotch.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Citrus orchards, backyard kitchen gardens, dry scrub, and woodland edges.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Widespread across Tamil Nadu orchards, home gardens, and foothills including Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, Dindigul, and Nilgiri slopes.

Seasonality: Common year-round statewide, with dry-season clouded forms appearing from December to April.

Flight & behaviour

Flutters persistently around tender shoots of lime and curry-leaf relatives; basks with spread wings on sunny foliage and sips nectar at garden blooms.

Field note: Larvae feed on tender Rutaceae shoots and are regularly tended by Camponotus and Crematogaster ants; seasonal dry-winter forms display a dramatic dark discal cloud below.
