Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Silver Forget-me-not (Catochrysops panormus) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 4 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Scarcer silvery-white relative with the costal dot shifted to the band

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are bright silvery lilac-blue (slightly frostier and paler than Catochrysops strabo) with a fine dark marginal thread; females are brown with pale bluish-white central areas and dark hindwing submarginal spots.

Underwing: Pale silvery-grey to off-white with crisp white-bordered buff bands where the small forewing costal spot sits adjacent to (or touching) the upper end of the postdiscal band, sharper chevron-shaped submarginal lunules, an orange-capped black eyespot in space 2, and a fine tail.

Lookalikes: Distinguished from Forget-me-not (Catochrysops strabo) by its silvery-white ventral ground colour and the forewing costal spot lying close against the postdiscal band rather than midway to the cell-end bar.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Moist and dry forest edges, wooded foothills, coastal scrub-forests, and grassy clearings.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Localized across Tamil Nadu in scattered Western Ghats foothills and wooded coastal pockets including Kalakkad-Mundanthurai, Siruvani, Anamalais, Mudumalai, and Chennai-Chengalpattu scrub.

Seasonality: Uncommon and patchy, mostly noted from August to February.

Flight & behaviour

Swift erratic flight close to woodland herbs and flowering shrubs; settles sharply on sunlit grass blades and damp soil.

Field note: Mainland Indian populations belong to subspecies Catochrysops panormus exiguus; often passes unnoticed among swarms of common Catochrysops strabo unless underside spots are checked closely.
