Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Pyrginae (Flats, Angles & Grizzled Skippers)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Pyrginae): Skippers that commonly settle with wings spread flat against leaves or tucked underneath foliage.

Typical habitats: Forest trails, rocky streams, dry open grasslands, and hill clearings.

Field photography tip: Dorsal open-wing photos clearly reveal white hyaline forewing and hindwing spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Common Snow Flat (Tagiades japetus) என்பது தாவிகள் (விரிசிறகுத் தாவிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 80 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Dusky brown snow flat lacking a pure white upper hindwing patch

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark chocolate-brown forewings dotted with crisp tiny hyaline white spots (cell, discal spaces, and subapical arc); hindwings stay entirely brown above with only faint darker post-discal spots and pale cilia near the tornus.

Underwing: Buff-brown forewing repeating the white dots; hindwing heavily washed with chalky greyish-white over the lower two-thirds and dotted with a curved arc of dark brown spots.

Lookalikes: Unlike Water Snow Flat (Tagiades litigiosa) and Suffused Snow Flat (Tagiades gana), the South Indian subspecies lacks a stark white patch on the upper hindwing.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Evergreen, moist deciduous, and bamboo forests, shaded estate edges, and secondary woodlands.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Nilgiris, Mudumalai, Siruvani, Kallar, Valparai, Kodaikanal slopes, Meghamalai, and Eastern Ghats (Yercaud, Kolli Hills, Pachamalai).

Seasonality: Common year-round across wet hills and foothills.

Flight & behaviour

Darts briskly along sunlit gaps before snapping flat beneath a broad leaf with wings held horizontally; males patrol fixed leaf perches and visit bird droppings.

Field note: Represented in Tamil Nadu by subspecies Tagiades japetus obscurus, whose dark brown upper hindwing makes the chalky white underside flash unexpectedly in flight.
