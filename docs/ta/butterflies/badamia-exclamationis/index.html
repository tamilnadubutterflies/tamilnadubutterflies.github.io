Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Coeliadinae (Awls, Awlets & Awlkings)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Coeliadinae): Large, swift skippers active particularly at dawn, dusk, and shaded forest interiors; several possess metallic-green or bright orange markings.

Typical habitats: Dense hill forests, bamboo patches, shaded ravines, and coastal scrub.

Field photography tip: Focus on the underside hindwing white/blue bands and dark tornal spots. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் பழுப்புத் தாவி. Brown Awl (Badamia exclamationis) என்பது தாவிகள் (பெருந்தாவிகள் மற்றும் நாமத்தாவிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 71 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Pointed-wing swift awl known for sudden mass arrivals

Identification marks

Upperwing: Elongated, narrow-pointed chocolate-brown wings marked on the forewing with fine translucent white exclamation-mark spots.

Underwing: Pale greyish-brown with a dark spot edged white near the hindwing inner corner.

Lookalikes: Extremely narrow pointed forewings and slender exclamation-like translucent streaks separate it immediately from Hasora awls.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Wooded foothills, parks, avenues, coastal forests, and dry hill trails.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Recorded across plains, foothills, and hill forests throughout Tamil Nadu.

Seasonality: Year-round with sudden seasonal population surges.

Flight & behaviour

Lightning-fast flight; rests under leaves in shade and is often active early morning and late afternoon.

Field note: Capable of long-distance migratory flights across peninsular India during monsoon shifts.
