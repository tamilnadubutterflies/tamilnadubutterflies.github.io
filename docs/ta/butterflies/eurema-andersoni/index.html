Field guide notes for Pieridae · Coliadinae (Yellows, Emigrants & Clouded Yellows)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Coliadinae): Yellow, pale green, or orange butterflies with rapid sustained flight and strong migratory behaviour in several species.

Typical habitats: Open grasslands, agricultural margins, scrub, riverbeds, and Nilgiri/Palani high-altitude meadows (Colias).

Field photography tip: For Eurema grass yellows, zoom into the underside forewing cell to count the small dark cell spots. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

One-spot Grass Yellow (Eurema andersoni) என்பது வெள்ளையன்கள் (புல் மஞ்சள் மற்றும் கொன்னை வெள்ளையன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 36 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Forest grass yellow marked by a single spot inside the forewing cell

Identification marks

Upperwing: Bright sulphur-yellow with a broad jet-black forewing outer border excavated cleanly inside spaces 2 and 3 and rounded along the hindwing edge.

Underwing: Paler yellow speckled lightly with rusty markings; forewing underside cell holds just a single small comma or spot plus faint apical streaks in dry forms.

Lookalikes: Separated from Common Grass Yellow (two cell spots) and Three-spot Grass Yellow (three cell spots) by having a single cell spot on the underside forewing and strictly forest habitat.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Shaded wet evergreen and moist deciduous forests along damp tracks and stream banks.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Western Ghats districts of Tamil Nadu: Nilgiris, Siruvani, Anamalai, Kodaikanal slopes, and KMTR.

Seasonality: Recorded mainly between July and February in humid Western Ghats valleys.

Flight & behaviour

Flies close to shaded forest undergrowth and settles softly on sunlit stream banks to sip dissolved minerals.

Field note: Unlike lawn-dwelling grass yellows, caterpillars feed on the woody rhamnaceous liana Ventilago rather than Cassia legumes.
