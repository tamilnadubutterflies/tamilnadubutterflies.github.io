Field guide notes for Pieridae · Pierinae (Whites, Orange-tips & Jezebels)

Family overview (Pieridae, Whites and yellows): Medium-to-small butterflies dominated by white, yellow, orange, and black pigments, ranging from garden grass yellows and emigrants to hill-dwelling clouded yellows and forest albatrosses.

Subfamily traits (Pierinae): Includes Delias jezebels, Cepora gulls, Appias albatrosses/puffins, Colotis/Ixias orange-tips, Hebomoia, and Prioneris.

Typical habitats: Spans dry scrub plains (Colotis), urban avenues (Delias), and wet evergreen hills (Appias, Prioneris).

Field photography tip: Capture forewing tip markings and underside hindwing vein patterns. Capture the closed-wing underside when possible, spot counts in Eurema and wingbar markings in Appias and Ixias are key underside clues.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Lesser Gull (Cepora nadina) என்பது வெள்ளையன்கள் (வெள்ளையன்கள் மற்றும் ஆரஞ்சு நுனிச்சிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 17 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Hill-forest gull with soft greenish-ochre unstreaked underside hindwings

Identification marks

Upperwing: Pure white in males (heavily dusted blackish-brown in females) with broad black outer forewing border indented gently on its inner edge.

Underwing: Hindwings smoothly washed in pale brownish-yellow to olive-green without dark outlines tracing every vein.

Lookalikes: Lacks the bold dark-veined grid found beneath the Common Gull (Cepora nerissa), and lacks the crimson hindwing underside spots of Painted Sawtooth.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Evergreen and moist deciduous hill forests, forest streams, and shola glades.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Western Ghats (Nilgiris, Mudumalai, Siruvani, Anamalai, Palani Hills) and localized in Eastern Ghats foothill streams.

Seasonality: Mostly September to April in humid hill ranges.

Flight & behaviour

Glides tirelessly along forested streams, dropping quickly onto damp sand patches alongside Puffins and Albatrosses.

Field note: Highlighted as an uncommon forest sighting at Amaidhi Solai foothills of Palani Hills (Pattampoochi Issue 24).
