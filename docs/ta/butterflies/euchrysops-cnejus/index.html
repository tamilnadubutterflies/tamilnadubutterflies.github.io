Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் பயறு நீலன். Gram Blue (Euchrysops cnejus) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 613 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

WPA-protected pulse blue flashing twin orange-capped tornal eyespots

Identification marks

Upperwing: Males are pale violet-lilac blue with narrow dark borders and two prominent black marginal spots in hindwing spaces 1b and 2; females are dark brown with bright blue basal suffusion and orange-crowned black hindwing marginal spots.

Underwing: Pale buff-grey patterned with crisp white-rimmed brown bands, a small black costal spot near the forewing mid-costa, two jet-black sub-basal spots on the hindwing, two bright orange-crowned black tornal eyespots flecked with metallic green, and a short thread-like tail.

Lookalikes: Separated from Forget-me-not (Catochrysops strabo) by two black basal hindwing dots and two equal orange-crowned tornal eyespots rather than one main eyespot in space 2, and from Plains Cupid (Chilades pandava) by larger size and buff-grey tone.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Pulse farmlands, fallow scrub, urban gardens, dry grasslands, and hill foothills.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Abundant across every district of Tamil Nadu from coastal plains and delta croplands to Nilgiri and Eastern Ghats foothills.

Seasonality: Common year-round statewide, peaking during post-monsoon cropping seasons.

Flight & behaviour

Brisk low flight over flowering gram fields and open weeds; perches frequently on legume pods and visits damp soil.

Field note: Despite legal protection under India's Wildlife (Protection) Act, it thrives across agricultural landscapes; caterpillars bore inside pulse pods guarded by Camponotus and Crematogaster ants.
