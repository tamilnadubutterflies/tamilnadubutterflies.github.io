Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Theclinae (Silverlines, Oakblues, Royals, Flashes & Yamfly)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Theclinae): Often tailed hairstreaks with metallic silver bars (Cigaritis), deep violet-blue upperwings (Arhopala), or swift red-and-slate flashes (Rapala).

Typical habitats: Scrub jungles, forest canopies, mistletoe-bearing trees, and rocky hilltops.

Field photography tip: Capture the hindwing tornal lobes, tail count, and arrangement of underside bands. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் மேகமலை வெள்ளிவரையன். Cloud Forest Silverline (Cigaritis meghamalaiensis) என்பது நீலன்கள் (வெள்ளிவரையன்கள் மற்றும் அரசநீலன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 3 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Recently described Western Ghats silverline from Meghamalai hills

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark brown wings extensively marked with iridescent metallic blue across both forewing and hindwing.

Underwing: Buff-yellow crossed by metallic silver-centered dark bands: forewing discal and post-discal bands lie parallel from the costa, and the hindwing post-basal band is unbroken.

Lookalikes: Diagnosed from all other Western Ghats Cigaritis (Spindasis) silverlines by the parallel conjoined forewing bands and continuous hindwing post-basal band ending at vein 1b.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Montane cloud forest clearings and edges in the southern Western Ghats.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Meghamalai Hills (Theni district, Tamil Nadu), the type locality of this newly described species.

Seasonality: Documented around monsoon and post-monsoon months in high hills.

Flight & behaviour

Observed basking briefly on low vegetation along cloud-forest hill slopes.

Field note: Described by S.R.K. Naicker, K. Sadasivan & colleagues in ENTOMON (2023) and formally added to the Tamil Nadu Butterfly Checklist.
