Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Miletinae (Apefly (Carnivorous Lycaenids))

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Miletinae): Represented in Tamil Nadu by the Apefly (Spalgis epius), whose caterpillars feed on mealybugs rather than plants.

Typical habitats: Gardens, orchards, scrub, and forest edges near scale-insect colonies.

Field photography tip: Underside pale grey wings crossed by numerous fine wavy brown striations distinguish the Apefly. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Apefly (Spalgis epius) என்பது நீலன்கள் (மாவுப்பூச்சி உண்ணும் சிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 167 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Unique carnivorous lycaenid whose caterpillars prey on plant mealybugs

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark smoky brown bearing a small pale white square patch near the middle of the forewing.

Underwing: Silvery grey-white intricately scribbled with dozens of fine, wavy dark-brown lines.

Lookalikes: Fine scribbled grey-white underside lacks distinct black dots, bands, or tails, completely distinct from all grass blues.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Home gardens, orchards, coffee/guava farms, and forest edges hosting mealybug colonies.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Found quietly across Tamil Nadu gardens, farms, and forests.

Seasonality: Year-round statewide.

Flight & behaviour

Hovers briskly around mealybug-infested hibiscus, citrus, or guava shoots; its pupa remarkably resembles a tiny rhesus-monkey face.

Field note: Purely entomophagous caterpillars prey directly on scale insects and citrus mealybugs instead of eating plants.
