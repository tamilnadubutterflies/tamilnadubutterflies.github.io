Field guide notes for Hesperiidae · Hesperiinae (Grass Skippers, Swifts, Aces & Darts)

Family overview (Hesperiidae, Skippers): Compact, fast-darting butterflies (83 checklist species) such as awls, flats, angles, aces, bobs, demons, swifts, and grass darts.

Subfamily traits (Hesperiinae): Fast-flying tawny, brown, or black-and-yellow skippers that typically hold forewings and hindwings at different angles when basking.

Typical habitats: Grasslands, paddy margins, palms, bamboo thickets, and forest undergrowth.

Field photography tip: Zoom into the underside hindwing spot pattern and forewing cell spots to distinguish swifts and darts. Photograph both the upperwing hyaline spots and the underside hindwing spot arcs, essential for distinguishing swifts and aces.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

Hampson's Hedge Hopper (Baracus hampsoni) என்பது தாவிகள் (புல்தாவிகள் மற்றும் தென்னைத்தாவிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 4 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Foothill Western Ghats hedge hopper marked with broken pale spots underneath

Identification marks

Upperwing: Dark umber-brown dusted olive at the wing base, displaying small discrete pale yellowish discal and subapical forewing spots (brighter in females) and plain dark hindwings.

Underwing: Warm ochreous-brown on the hindwing broken into distinct rows of separated pale creamy-yellow spots and dark-edged blotches rather than unbroken longitudinal stripes.

Lookalikes: Spot-broken under-hindwing pattern separates it from the cleanly striped Hedge Hopper (Baracus subditus), and it lacks the bright yellow underside of Bush Hopper (Ampittia dioscorides).

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Mid-elevation forest grasslands, bamboo clearings, moist deciduous edges, and grassy stream banks from 300 to 1,200 m.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Mid-elevation Western Ghats slopes of Tamil Nadu including Siruvani, Anamalai foothills, Mudumalai, and Kalakkad-Mundanthurai.

Seasonality: Recorded chiefly from August to January during monsoon and post-monsoon grass growth.

Flight & behaviour

Hops low through damp grass blades, basking in jet-plane posture in morning sun and nectaring at small ground herbs such as Justicia and Ageratum.

Field note: An intermediate population described from the Anamalai Hills as form gotha bridges variation between B. hampsoni and B. subditus.
