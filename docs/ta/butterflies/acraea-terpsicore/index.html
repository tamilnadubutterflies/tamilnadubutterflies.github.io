Field guide notes for Nymphalidae · Heliconiinae (Fritillaries, Lacewings, Leopards, Coster & Yeoman)

Family overview (Nymphalidae, Brush-footed butterflies): Tamil Nadu’s largest butterfly family (97 checklist species), encompassing tigers, crows, pansies, eggflies, sailers, barons, rajahs, browns, and Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman.

Subfamily traits (Heliconiinae): Bright orange-tawny butterflies with dark spots, serrated margins, or pearlescent underside bands, including Tamil Nadu’s state butterfly, Tamil Yeoman.

Typical habitats: Sunny clearings, grasslands, montane sholas, and forest paths.

Field photography tip: Compare hindwing underside bands and marginal scalloping to separate similar orange species. Record both basking upperside and resting underside views, especially for bushbrowns, treebrowns, sailers, and oakleaf butterflies.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

இதன் தமிழ்ப் பெயர் செம்பழுப்பு வரியன். Tawny Coster (Acraea terpsicore) என்பது வரியன்கள் (தமிழ் மறவன் மற்றும் சிறுத்தைச் சிறகிகள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 2,114 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Slender brick-orange butterfly of sunlit lawns and grasslands

Identification marks

Upperwing: Narrow brick-orange wings dotted with neat black spots and bordered by a black hindwing margin spotted in white.

Underwing: Paler orange-pink with glossy white marginal spots inside a black border on the hindwing.

Lookalikes: Elongated wings, black-and-white spotted hindwing margin, and long narrow abdomen distinguish it from leopards and fritillaries.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: Sunny lawns, open fields, dry scrub, roadsides, and fallow gardens.

Tamil Nadu distribution: Common across Tamil Nadu plains, coasts, and open hills.

Seasonality: Abundant year-round across open landscapes.

Flight & behaviour

Weak, low leisurely flight; settles openly on grass blades and Tridax flowers.

Field note: Oozes bitter yellow defensive fluid when disturbed; females carry a leathery mating plug (sphragis) after pairing.
