Field guide notes for Lycaenidae · Polyommatinae (Grass Blues, Pierrots, Lineblues & Ceruleans)

Family overview (Lycaenidae, Blues and hairstreaks): Small, jewel-like butterflies (96 checklist species) including grass blues, pierrots, lineblues, ceruleans, silverlines, oakblues, royals, flashes, and sunbeams.

Subfamily traits (Polyommatinae): Delicate small blues with distinctive underside spot chains, zebra stripes, or white-and-black pied markings.

Typical habitats: Lawns, grasslands, damp sand, woodland edges, and hill paths across all elevations.

Field photography tip: Macro underside views showing hindwing sub-basal spots and tornal orange eyespots are essential. Sharp macro shots of the hindwing underside bands and spot alignments are usually far more diagnostic than upperside blue.

தமிழ் அறிமுகம் & அடையாள சுருக்கம்:

White Hedge Blue (Udara akasa) என்பது நீலன்கள் (புல் நீலன்கள் மற்றும் விகடன்கள்) குடும்பத்தைச் சேர்ந்த தமிழ்நாடு பட்டாம்பூச்சியாகும். iNaturalist தமிழ்நாடு திட்டத்தில் இதுவரை 61 பதிவுகள் பதிவாகியுள்ளன.

விரிவான இறக்கை அடையாளக் குறிப்புகள் ஆங்கிலத்தில்:

Chalk-white shola hedge blue fluttering along high mountain streams

Identification marks

Upperwing: Tailless; males are extensively chalky white across both wings with wide black forewing apical-costal borders and soft bright blue iridescence restricted strictly to wing bases; females are overwhelmingly white with broad black borders and almost no blue.

Underwing: Pure crisp white sparsely etched with tiny black postdiscal dots, thin cell-end bars, and faint marginal dots, lacking prominent dark submarginal zigzag crescents.

Lookalikes: Separated immediately from White-disc Hedge Blue (Celatoxia albidisca) by its extensive chalk-white upperwing across most of both wings and pure white, delicately dotted underside.

Habitat & Tamil Nadu range

Habitats: High-elevation shola-grassland margins, montane streams, and wet evergreen paths above 1,200 m.

Tamil Nadu distribution: High-elevation Western Ghats peaks of Tamil Nadu including the Nilgiris (Ooty, Avalanche, Kotagiri), Palani Hills (Kodaikanal), Valparai, Anamalai, Megamalai, and Kalakkad-Mundanthurai.

Seasonality: Active year-round in high hills, peaking from September to April.

Flight & behaviour

Gentle fluttering flight at low to middle height around stream-side Persicaria thickets; males puddle steadily on sunlit wet sand.

Field note: Represented in the southern Indian hills by endemic subspecies Udara akasa mavisa; its association with Persicaria chinensis keeps adults anchored near cool shola streams.
